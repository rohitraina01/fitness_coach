⚡ APEX COACH OPERATING INSTRUCTIONS

Your complete cheatsheet for controlling your fitness coaching system, mobile bridge, and Antigravity agent from your phone and PC.

📁 1. The Command Center (commands/ Folder)

All automation scripts live in the commands/ folder (and have root forwarders):

Batch Script What It Does
start_server.bat Launches the bridge server on port 8080. Prints your local IP. Closing the window terminates the server cleanly.
kill_server.bat Immediately frees port 8080 and kills the server process.
open_coach_session.bat Pulls latest workouts from GitHub and opens Antigravity IDE agent session on your laptop.
push_to_github.bat Stages all records, auto-commits, and pushes to remote GitHub repository.
pull_from_github.bat Syncs cloud workouts down to your laptop.
rebuild_dashboard.bat Regenerates fitness_dashboard.xlsx and mobile cards.
run_integrity_audit.bat Runs automated audit across all CSVs and logs.

📱 2. Accessing from Your Phone

A
Same Home Wi-Fi: Double-click commands/start_server.bat. Open phone browser and navigate to http://<YOUR-PC-IP>:8080.
B
At Gym via Tailscale (Best): Install Tailscale on PC and phone (100% free). Open http://<PC-Tailscale-IP>:8080. Works 24/7 over cellular data.
C
Save as App: Tap Share ➔ Add to Home Screen on iPhone or Add to Home Screen on Android.

🏋️ 3. Workout Logging & Automated Git Push

1
On your phone, tap the 📝 Log tab. Type or tap 🎙️ Dictate to enter your sets.
2
Tap Process Workout ➔.
3
The laptop server ingests the workout, updates DATA/workouts.csv, updates Markdown logs, refreshes the Excel dashboard, runs git commit and pushes directly to GitHub master!
4
Your phone app refreshes automatically without you needing to reload!

🤖 4. Live Coach AI on the Gym Floor

1
Tap the 🤖 Coach AI tab in the phone app.
2
Ask any question (e.g. "Shoulder pinching on Incline press, what substitute?", "What is my next weight on Lat pulldown?") or send program change instructions.
3
Tap Send to Laptop ➔. You get instant evidence-based coach feedback AND see the live activity stream from your laptop agent as it inspects files, runs scripts, and commits changes!

⏳ 5. Smart Offline Queue (PC Turned Off)

If your PC is turned off while you are at the gym, both Workouts and Coach Prompts are safely stored in your phone's browser queue. When you boot up your PC, open the app and tap Sync to PC ➔ to process all items with zero duplicate records!
