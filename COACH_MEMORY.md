## 4. BIOMECHANICAL WEAKNESSES, BOTTLENECKS & GUARDRAILS

### A. Anterior Pelvic Tilt (APT) & 9-Hour Desk Posture
- **Root Cause**: 9 hours of seated desk work causes shortened hip flexors (psoas) and underactive deep abdominals (transverse abdominis), pulling pelvis forward and pushing belly/glutes out.
- **Strict Guardrail**: **ZERO Squats, ZERO Deadlifts, ZERO Hip Thrusts, ZERO Lunges**. Any exercise that builds gluteal mass is prohibited to avoid worsening feminine lower-body proportions.
- **Core Form Cue**: On all ab exercises (Floor Crunches, Lying Leg Raises, Deadbugs), the lower back must remain **glued 100% flat to the floor**. If the lower back arches even 1mm, the psoas took over.
