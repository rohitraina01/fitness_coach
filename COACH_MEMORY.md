## 4. BIOMECHANICAL WEAKNESSES, BOTTLENECKS & GUARDRAILS

### A. Anterior Pelvic Tilt (APT) & 9-Hour Desk Posture
- **Root Cause**: 9 hours of seated desk work causes shortened hip flexors (psoas) and underactive deep abdominals (transverse abdominis), pulling pelvis forward and pushing belly/glutes out.
- **Strict Guardrail**: **ZERO Squats, ZERO Deadlifts, ZERO Hip Thrusts, ZERO Lunges**. Any exercise that builds gluteal mass is prohibited to avoid worsening feminine lower-body proportions.
- **Core Form Cue**: On all ab exercises (Floor Crunches, Lying Leg Raises, Deadbugs), the lower back must remain **glued 100% flat to the floor**. If the lower back arches even 1mm, the psoas took over.

### B. Lateral Delt Hypertrophy (Slow Grower)
- **Root Cause**: Trainee historically struggled with lateral delt growth due to upper trapezius takeover.
- **Mandatory Mind-Muscle Cues**:
  1. *Knuckles to Far Walls*: Reach outwards toward the room corners, NOT straight up.
  2. *Elbows Apart*: Pull elbows apart like stretching an elastic band.
  3. *Pouring Water*: Keep pinkies level with thumbs.
  4. *Pocket Scapulae*: Depress shoulder blades down into back pockets; stop rep at collarbone height.

### C. Nutrition: The #1 Rate-Limiting Bottleneck
- **Target**: 1,850 kcal/day | **145–150 g protein floor** (~2.0 g/kg).
- **Historical Gap**: 30-day recorded average was **108.1 g/day**, with dips to 70g.
- **Directive**: This is the single biggest bottleneck capping aesthetic rate of transformation. Daily protein floor of 145g must be maintained every single day regardless of workout vs rest days.
