# COACHING & PROGRESSION RULES

## 1. DYNAMIC DOUBLE PROGRESSION & THE TOP-SET OVERLOAD MODEL
All hypertrophy exercises follow evidence-based progressive overload, calibrated to commercial gym equipment realities.

### The Mechanics: Top-Set + Back-Off Volume Protocol
For compound movements (e.g. Incline DB Press, Smith Machine Bench, Lat Pulldowns, Seated Rows):
1. **Set 1 (Heavy Overload Top-Set)**:
   - Primary driver of mechanical tension and high-threshold motor unit recruitment.
   - Assigned Rep Window: **6–10 reps @ 1–2 RIR**.
   - When you hit the top of the rep ceiling (10 clean reps), advance the load by the smallest possible increment in the next session.
2. **Sets 2–3 (Hypertrophy Volume Back-Off Sets)**:
   - Drop load by **10%–15%** (e.g. 22.5 kg → 20.0 kg, or 47.5 kg → 42.5 kg).
   - Assigned Rep Window: **8–12 reps @ 1–2 RIR**.
   - Accumulates high-tension metabolic volume without form breakdown or joint grind.

### Commercial Gym Increment Rules
1. **Dumbbells**:
   - The gym dumbbell rack has: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 12.5, 15, 17.5, 20, 22.5 kg (**NO 6.0 kg DB exists**).
   - A jump from 5 kg to 7 kg represents an aggressive **+40% overload**. For isolation movements (e.g. DB Lateral Raises), do NOT jump to 7 kg; instead, expand the rep accumulation window to **12–20 reps** with 2-second eccentrics, deploy dropsets, or shift heavy progressive overload to Cable Lateral Raises.
2. **Cable & Selectorized Machines**:
   - Light plates start at 2.5 kg increments, but main stacks transition strictly to **+5.0 kg increments** (no fractional 17.5 kg or 22.5 kg pins).
   - When a +5 kg leap is too steep for an isolation movement (e.g. Triceps Pushdowns 20 kg → 25 kg is a +25% jump), master the top rep ceiling (12–15 strict reps) and add a 1-second peak contraction hold before advancing the pin.

## 2. SET CLASSIFICATION & INTENSITY (RIR) STANDARDS

| Set Type | Code | Criteria & Inclusion | RIR Standard | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Warm-Up** | `W` | Submaximal prep sets. **Excluded from volume metrics.** | 4+ RIR | Neurological priming, joint synovial fluid distribution, grooving motor pattern. |
| **Working Set** | `S` | High mechanical tension, strict execution. **Included in volume.** | 1–2 RIR (Compounds), 0–1 RIR (Isolations) | Primary hypertrophy stimulus. High motor-unit recruitment. |
| **Drop Set** | `D` | Immediate load reduction after a working set with no rest. | 0 RIR | Metabolic stress, recruitment of fatigued fibers. Linked to parent set. |
| **Rest-Pause** | `RP` | Short 15s pause after reaching near-failure, followed by additional reps. | 0 RIR | Density training on high-stability isolations. |

### Failure Management Rules
- **Multi-Joint Free Weight Lifts** (Bench press, Incline DB press): Stop at **1–2 RIR**. Technical breakdown under heavy axial load produces fatigue and joint shear out of proportion to muscle gain.
- **Stable Machines & Isolations** (Cable lateral raises, Tricep pushdowns, Leg extensions, Pec deck): Safe to train to **0–1 RIR** or momentary muscular failure (inability to complete another concentric rep with standardized form).

## 3. SESSION DENSITY & WORKING SET CAPS (ANTI-MARATHON GUARDRAIL)
- **Session Duration Guardrail**: **50–55 minutes maximum**.
- **Working Set Cap**: Every session is hard-capped at **5 core exercises (14–16 total working sets max)**.
- **The Science**: Hypertrophy signaling plateaus after 6–8 hard sets per muscle group per session. Sets performed after minute 60 suffer from elevated cortisol, depleted glycogen, and diminished motor-unit recruitment. 15 savage sets beats 25 sloppy sets every time.
- **Rest Interval Standards**:
  - Primary Compound Lifts: **150–180 seconds** (full ATP-CP resynthesis).
  - Machine Compounds / Rows: **120 seconds**.
  - Isolation Accessories / Core: **60–90 seconds**.

## 4. HIGH-STABILITY BRACING MANDATE
Hypertrophy requires mechanical stability. When the torso or pelvis is wobbling, the nervous system down-regulates motor unit recruitment to the target muscle to protect the spine and joints.
- **Pulling Stability**: Unstable, seated freestanding cable rows are prohibited. Always prioritize:
  1. **Chest-Supported Row Machines** (sternum glued to pad, zero spinal torque).
  2. **Bench-Braced One-Arm Dumbbell Rows** (knee and hand planted firmly on flat bench).
  3. **Lifting Wrist Straps**: Mandatory on all vertical lat pulldowns and heavy rows to completely remove grip/forearm fatigue from the back equation.
- **Pressing Stability**: Bench set rigidly to 30° on Incline DB Press. Shoulders depressed down into back pockets before unracking.

## 5. HAMSTRING & ANTI-APT POSTURAL ARCHITECTURE
To reverse 9 hours of seated desk Anterior Pelvic Tilt (APT) without building unwanted glute mass:
- **Strict Anti-Glute Guardrail**: **ZERO Barbell Squats, ZERO Deadlifts, ZERO Hip Thrusts, ZERO Walking Lunges**. Glute hypertrophy is strictly prevented to keep the waist and hips narrow for maximum V-taper.
- **Hamstring Knee-Flexion Volume**: Quads must never overpower hamstrings. Hamstrings attach to the sit bones and pull the pelvis *backward* into neutral alignment. Maintain dedicated weekly volume on Lying or Seated Leg Curls with **toes dorsiflexed** (pulled up to shins) to shut off the calves and isolate the hamstrings.
- **Lower Back Floor Contact Rule**: On all abdominal exercises (Floor Crunches, Deadbugs, Leg Raises), the lumbar spine must remain **100% glued flat to the floor**. If the lower back arches even 1 millimeter, terminate the set immediately—the hip flexors (psoas) have taken over.

## 6. FATIGUE MANAGEMENT & DELOAD PROTOCOLS
- Do not take deloads arbitrarily every 4 weeks. Deloads are earned and indicated by data.
- **Deload Indicators**:
  1. Systematic performance regression across ≥ 2 consecutive workouts despite adequate sleep and nutrition.
  2. Joint tenderness or aching tendons (e.g. elbows, patellar, anterior shoulder).
  3. Persistent lethargy and loss of training motivation.
- **Deload Prescription**: Reduce volume by 50% (perform only 1–2 working sets per exercise) and maintain intensity at 2–3 RIR for 5 to 7 days.

## 7. MANDATORY MIND-MUSCLE CONNECTION (MMC) & VISCERAL FORM CUES
Every exercise prescribed in any session blueprint MUST include an anatomically precise Mind-Muscle Connection cue:
- **Lateral Deltoids**: Depress shoulder blades down first (kill upper traps). Lead with elbows in the scapular plane (15° forward of torso). Imagine pushing knuckles *out toward the walls* rather than lifting up. Zero swinging.
- **Lats & Rows**: Drive elbows down toward hip pockets. Treat hands as passive hooks. Full 2-second stretch at top without rounding spine.
- **Quads (Leg Press / Extensions)**: Narrow/low platform stance. Stop at 90° knee bend so pelvis stays glued and glutes stay asleep. On extensions, hold 1s peak lockout flexing vastus medialis, 3s negative.
- **Hamstrings (Leg Curls)**: Always dorsiflex feet (pull toes to shins) to shut off calves and force 100% hamstring loading. Zero lower back arch.
- **Calves (Calf Raises)**: 2-second dead stop in deep stretch to kill Achilles elastic bounce. Push through big toe ball.
- **Six-Pack (Floor Crunch)**: Lower back glued to floor. Roll spine into a tight ball, curling ribcage down to pelvic bowl. Exhale all oxygen at peak squeeze.

## 9. STRICT SPLIT BALANCING & ANTI-LEG-SKIPPING GOVERNANCE
- **Max Consecutive Upper-Body Cap (Strict 2-Session Limit)**:
  - The system strictly prohibits prescribing more than **2 consecutive upper-body sessions (Push or Pull)** without an intervening **Legs & Core** session.
