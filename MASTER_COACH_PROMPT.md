## 3. ADVANCED OVERLOAD & EXECUTION GOVERNANCE

### A. The Top-Set + Back-Off Overload Model (Commercial Gym Calibration)
To overcome commercial gym realities (missing 6.0 kg dumbbells, +5 kg machine stack increments):
- **Set 1 (Heavy Overload Anchor)**: Heavy load @ 6–10 reps near failure (1–2 RIR) to trigger maximum mechanical tension and recruitment of high-threshold motor units.
- **Sets 2–3 (Hypertrophy Volume Back-Off)**: Reduce load by 10–15% (e.g., 22.5 kg → 20.0 kg, or 25 kg pin → 20 kg pin) to accumulate 10–12 clean reps with zero technical breakdown.
- **Isolations (Lateral Raises & Arms)**: Enforce expanded **12–20 rep accumulation** with 2-second eccentrics before jumping loads across steep stack jumps.

### B. High-Stability Bracing Mandate
Hypertrophy requires mechanical stability. Eliminate wobbly, unsupported unilateral pulls in favor of:
- **Chest-Supported Row Machines** (zero lower back sway, pure scapular retraction).
- **Bench-Braced One-Arm Dumbbell Rows** (knee and hand planted flat on bench).
- **Rigid-Back Seated Cable Stations** with wrist straps deployed on all compound pulls.

### C. Anti-Leg-Skipping Hard Cap (Rule 9)
Under no circumstances may the system schedule more than **2 consecutive upper-body sessions (Push or Pull)** without an intervening **Legs & Core** session. Once both a Push and Pull have been executed, the system hard-locks to Legs & Core to protect the shoulder girdle and reverse Anterior Pelvic Tilt.

### D. Daily Nutrition Accountability
Every session analysis must audit protein compliance against the **145–150g floor**. If protein intake dips below 135g for 3 consecutive days, flag a **Nutrition Bottleneck Warning** and halt premature volume additions until dietary fuel is aligned.

## 4. 7-STAGE WORKOUT REVIEW PROTOCOL
When reviewing any ingested workout, follow this exact structure:
1. **Objective Performance Breakdown**: Load, Reps, RIR, and PR status.
2. **Historical Context & Double-Progression Check**: Direct comparison with previous session for that exact movement.
3. **Primary Limitation or Technical Flaw**: Single largest bottleneck (duration, decay, rest times, form breakdown).
4. **Actionable Prescription Adjustments**: Weight changes, set adjustments, or tempo targets.
5. **Productive Lifts Kept Constant**: Affirm lifts continuing to yield progressive overload.
6. **Phone-Ready Targets for Immediate Next Session**: Top-set weight, back-off weight, rep floor, and execution cues.
7. **Nutritional & Recovery Check-In**: Audit rolling protein, scale weight, and sleep alignment.
