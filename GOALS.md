# PHYSIQUE GOALS & STRATEGIC ROADMAP

## 1. VISION: THE ATHLETIC AESTHETIC PHYSIQUE
The primary objective is an athletic, tapered, muscular physique characterized by:
- Wide, 3D capped deltoids and a dense, wide upper back creating an unmistakable V-taper.
- Dense upper chest shelf filling out shirts and collarbones cleanly.
- Balanced arm hypertrophy (15+ inch arms lean) with distinct tricep horseshoe and bicep peak.
- A lean, flat midsection with clear abdominal definition at a sustainable body fat level (~12–14%).
- Athletic leg development (quad sweep, defined hamstrings and calves) that supports an athletic profile without dominating jeans sizing.

## 3. PHASED ROADMAP

### PHASE 1: PRIMING & RECOMPOSITION / MILD DEFICIT (Months 1–3: Oct – Dec 2026)
- **Primary Goal**: Solidify training consistency (5 days/week), establish calibrated baseline records for every exercise, increase daily steps from 6,000 to 8,500, and achieve a steady 0.3–0.5 kg/week fat reduction while building initial muscle mass.
- **Calorie Target**: 1,850 kcal/day (~300 kcal deficit below estimated maintenance).
- **Protein Target**: 145–150 g/day (~2.0 g/kg).
- **Target Scale Weight**: Transition from ~73 kg to ~70 kg while maintaining or increasing lifting loads.
- **Key Milestones**:
  - Incline DB Press: Progression to 24–26 kg dumbbells for clean sets of 8–10 reps.
  - Lat Pulldown: Strict full-ROM working sets with bodyweight-equivalent resistance.
  - Lateral Raise: Strict strict-form sets with 10–12.5 kg dumbbells or full-stack control on cables.

### PHASE 2: TARGETED CUT & DEFINITION (Months 4–5: Jan – Feb 2027)
- **Primary Goal**: Reveal abdominal definition and upper-body muscular separation.
- **Calorie Target**: 1,700–1,800 kcal/day with step counts pushed to 10,000/day.
- **Target Scale Weight**: ~67–68 kg at ~12–13% body fat.

### PHASE 3: LEAN LEAN-BULK / HYPERTROPHY SURPLUS (Months 6+: Mar 2027+)
- **Primary Goal**: Controlled surplus (+200–250 kcal/day, gaining ~0.8–1.0 kg/month) to pack dense muscle mass into the deltoids, lats, and arms over 6–9 continuous months.
