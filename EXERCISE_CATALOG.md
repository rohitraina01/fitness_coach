# EXERCISE CATALOG & BIOMECHANICAL DIRECTORY

## 1. UPPER PUSH (CHEST, LATERAL/ANTERIOR DELTS, TRICEPS)

### 1.1 Incline Dumbbell Bench Press (`INCLINE_DB_PRESS`)
- **Primary Movers**: Clavicular Head Pectoralis Major (Upper Chest), Anterior Deltoid.
- **Secondary Movers**: Sternal Pectoralis, Triceps Brachii.
- **Bench Angle**: Set bench to 30° (or second notch). Avoid 45°+ which shifts load to anterior deltoids.
- **Execution Cues**: Retract and depress scapulae. Lower dumbbells with elbows tucked ~45–60° from torso until deep pec stretch. Press in a slight inward arc without clacking weights.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 8–12 reps. Increment by +2 kg per hand.

### 1.2 Dumbbell Lateral Raise (`DB_LATERAL_RAISE`)
- **Primary Movers**: Lateral Deltoid.
- **Secondary Movers**: Supraspinatus, Upper Trapezius.
- **Execution Cues**: Lean slightly forward (~10–15°). Raise dumbbells in scapular plane (30° forward of coronal plane). Lead with elbows, pouring water slightly at the apex. Do not shrug upper traps. Stop at shoulder height.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 10–15 reps. Increment by +1–2 kg per hand.

### 1.3 Cable Lateral Raise (`CABLE_LATERAL_RAISE`)
- **Primary Movers**: Lateral Deltoid.
- **Advantage Over DB**: Constant mechanical tension across entire range, especially in the lengthened position.
- **Execution Cues**: Set pulley at wrist/hip height. Cross cable behind back or in front. Drive elbow out and upward. Slow 2-second eccentric.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 12–15 reps.

### 1.4 Flat Barbell Bench Press (`FLAT_BB_BENCH`)
- **Primary Movers**: Sternal Pectoralis Major.
- **Secondary Movers**: Anterior Deltoid, Triceps Brachii.
- **Execution Cues**: Plant feet firmly. Moderate arch. Grip bar slightly wider than shoulder width. Touch lower sternum with control, press back and up. Maintain 1–2 RIR for safety.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 6–10 reps. Increment by +2.5 kg total.

### 1.5 Pec Deck Machine Fly (`PEC_DECK_FLY`)
- **Primary Movers**: Pectoralis Major (Sternal & Costal Heads).
- **Execution Cues**: Adjust seat so handles align with mid-chest. Keep elbows slightly bent but rigid. Squeeze chest hard at peak contraction for 1s pause, controlled 3s eccentric.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 10–15 reps.

### 1.6 Overhead Cable Triceps Extension (`OVERHEAD_TRICEPS_EXT`)
- **Primary Movers**: Triceps Brachii (Long Head).
- **Advantage**: Stretches the long head over the shoulder joint in full shoulder flexion.
- **Execution Cues**: Set cable to chest/shoulder height. Step forward, lean torso. Flare elbows slightly and extend forearms forward. Emphasize deep stretch behind head.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 10–12 reps.

### 1.7 Cable Triceps Pushdown (`TRICEPS_PUSHDOWN` / `ROPE_PUSHDOWN`)
- **Primary Movers**: Triceps Brachii (Lateral & Medial Heads).
- **Execution Cues**: Pin upper arms against ribcage. Drive bar/rope down until full elbow lockout. Control the return to 90° elbow flexion.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 10–15 reps.

### 1.8 Dumbbell Front Raise (`DB_FRONT_RAISE`)
- **Primary Movers**: Anterior Deltoid.
- **Note**: Generally redundant when heavy incline pressing and flat pressing are performed. Minimized or removed in balanced hypertrophy programs.

## 2. UPPER PULL (LATS, UPPER BACK, REAR DELTS, BICEPS)

### 2.1 Lat Pulldown (`LAT_PULLDOWN`)
- **Primary Movers**: Latissimus Dorsi.
- **Secondary Movers**: Teres Major, Biceps Brachii, Brachialis.
- **Execution Cues**: Moderate or neutral grip. Slight torso lean (~10°). Drive elbows straight down toward hips. Do not excessively yank torso back. Full stretch at top.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 8–12 reps.

### 2.2 Pull-Up (`PULL_UP`)
- **Primary Movers**: Latissimus Dorsi, Teres Major, Biceps Brachii.
- **Execution Cues**: Overhand or neutral grip. Dead hang stretch at bottom. Pull chin above bar leading with chest up. Avoid swinging or kipping.
- **Progression Model**: Rep progression up to 10 reps, then add external load belt.

### 2.3 Seated Cable Row (`SEATED_CABLE_ROW`)
- **Primary Movers**: Latissimus Dorsi, Mid-Trapezius, Rhomboids.
- **Execution Cues**: Close neutral grip (V-bar) or medium width. Pull handle into lower abdomen / belly button. Retract shoulder blades at finish, let scapulae protract under stretch.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 8–12 reps.

### 2.4 Chest-Supported Row Machine (`CHEST_SUPPORTED_ROW`)
- **Primary Movers**: Mid Trapezius, Rhomboids, Posterior Deltoids.
- **Advantage**: Eliminates lower back spinal erector fatigue, isolating the scapular retractors.
- **Execution Cues**: Flared elbow path (~45–60° from torso). Squeeze scapulae together hard at peak contraction.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 8–12 reps.

### 2.5 Straight-Arm Cable Pulldown (`STRAIGHT_ARM_PULLDOWN`)
- **Primary Movers**: Latissimus Dorsi (Isolated elbow extension avoided), Teres Major.
- **Execution Cues**: Soft bend in elbows, hinged hips. Sweep bar down in an arc into upper thighs. Feel intense lat contraction at the bottom.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 12–15 reps.

### 2.6 Reverse Pec Deck / Rear Delt Fly (`REAR_DELT_FLY`)
- **Primary Movers**: Posterior Deltoid.
- **Execution Cues**: Seat adjusted so handles align with shoulders. Arms almost straight. Sweep outwards horizontally without pinching scapulae together excessively.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 12–15 reps.

### 2.7 Standing Cable Bicep Curl (`CABLE_CURL`)
- **Primary Movers**: Biceps Brachii (Short & Long Heads).
- **Execution Cues**: Pin elbows by sides. Curl handle toward chin. Constant cable tension through the bottom range.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 10–12 reps.

### 2.8 Dumbbell Hammer Curl (`HAMMER_CURL`)
- **Primary Movers**: Brachioradialis, Brachialis, Biceps Long Head.
- **Advantage**: Builds arm thickness and forearm size.
- **Execution Cues**: Neutral grip (palms facing each other). Controlled curl without swinging shoulders.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 8–12 reps.

## 3. LOWER BODY & CORE (QUADS, HAMSTRINGS, GLUTES, CALVES, ABS)

### 3.1 Leg Press (`LEG_PRESS`)
- **Primary Movers**: Quadriceps, Gluteus Maximus.
- **Execution Cues**: Feet shoulder-width mid-platform. Lower sled to deep 90° knee flexion without lower back lifting off the pad. Press through mid-foot.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 8–12 reps.

### 3.2 Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift (`RDL`)
- **Primary Movers**: Hamstrings, Gluteus Maximus, Erector Spinae.
- **Execution Cues**: Push hips backward, keeping dumbbells gliding down shins. Slight knee bend. Stop when hamstrings reach full stretch. Snap hips forward.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 8–12 reps.

### 3.3 Seated or Lying Leg Curl (`LEG_CURL`)
- **Primary Movers**: Hamstrings (Knee Flexion).
- **Execution Cues**: Dorsiflex feet. Curl to touch pad to glutes. Slow 3-second eccentric return.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 10–12 reps.

### 3.4 Standing Calf Raise (`CALF_RAISE`)
- **Primary Movers**: Gastrocnemius, Soleus.
- **Execution Cues**: Pause 2 seconds in the full deep stretch at bottom to kill the Achilles tendon stretch reflex. Explosive plantarflexion, 1s peak squeeze.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 12–15 reps.

### 3.5 Cable Kneeling Crunch (`CABLE_CRUNCH`)
- **Primary Movers**: Rectus Abdominis.
- **Execution Cues**: Kneel in front of high cable rope. Lock hips in space. Curl torso like rolling a carpet, bringing elbows toward knees.
- **Progression Model**: Double progression, 12–15 reps.
