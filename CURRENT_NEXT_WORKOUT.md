- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 8–12 reps** | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**

### 2. Seated Cable Row (V-Bar Grip)
- **Order Rationale**: **#2 Mid-Back & Lower Lat Thickness.** Heavy horizontal pulling with zero lower back shear.
