## WORKOUT BLUEPRINT & EXERCISE TARGETS

### 1. Close-Grip / V-Bar Lat Pulldown
- **Order Rationale**: **#1 Vertical Sagittal Lat Mass Driver.** Recruits lower lats through shoulder extension with zero spinal torque while fresh.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 8–10 reps** | Rest: 120 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
- **Target Weight**: **45.0 kg – 50.0 kg pin**
  - *Floor Target*: 45.0 kg × 10, 10, 10 reps (clean baseline volume).
  - *Main Target*: Set 1 @ 50.0 kg (8–10 reps); Sets 2–3 @ 45.0 kg (10 reps).
  - *Stretch Target*: 50.0 kg × 10, 10, 8 reps across all 3 sets.
- **Warm-Up Ramp**: 30.0 kg × 10 reps, 40.0 kg × 6 reps with straps before working sets.
- **Form Cue**: Thigh pad locked firmly against quads so hips cannot rise. Lean back only 10–15° (no swinging). Drive elbows straight down toward your hips. Full controlled stretch at the top without letting shoulders shrug to ears.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Pretend your hands are passive hooks. Pull entirely through the tips of your elbows. Squeeze lower lats hard at bottom chest touch.

- **Form Cue**: Place left knee and left hand firmly on a flat bench. Right foot braced wide on floor. Back flat and parallel to floor. Grip dumbbell in right hand.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Pull the dumbbell smoothly in an arcing path toward your hip pocket, not up into your armpit. Feel the lat stretch at the bottom without letting your torso rotate.

- **EZ-Bar Curl Cue**: Pin elbows to sides of ribs. Curl up smoothly without swinging torso. Lower under a strict 3-second negative.
- **Hammer Curl Cue**: Immediately grab dumbbells with neutral (palms facing each other) grip. Curl toward shoulders. Squeezes brachialis and brachioradialis to push bicep peak upward.

### 6. Anti-APT Core Finisher: Floor Crunch & Deadbugs Superset
- **Order Rationale**: **#6 Transverse Abdominis & Postural Restoration.** Directly counters tight psoas from yesterday's trek; zero quad involvement.
- **Working Sets**: **3 supersets (12–15 crunches immediately into 6–8 deadbugs per side)** | Rest: 45 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
- **Target Weight**: **Bodyweight (0.0 kg)**
- **Floor Crunch Execution**:
  - Lie on back, knees bent 90°, feet flat on floor.
  - **Press lower back 100% flat into the floor (zero gap under lumbar spine)**.
  - Hands lightly touching temples. Curl your ribcage toward your pelvis; exhale every drop of air from your lungs at peak crunch. Hold peak contraction for 1 full second.
- **Deadbug Execution**:
  - Immediately raise knees to 90° tabletop position, arms pointing straight up.
  - Maintain absolute lumbar contact with the ground.
  - Slowly extend right arm overhead while extending left leg straight out hovering 2 inches off floor. Return and alternate.
  - *CUE*: If lower back arches off floor, stop extension immediately. Quality over reach.
