## COACHING BRIEFING & PROTOCOLS

### 1. Why Interleaved Sequencing (No Back-to-Back Chest Presses)
- **Active Chest & Triceps Clearance**: Running Incline DB Press and Smith Bench back-to-back leaves local metabolites (H+ ions, triceps fatigue) in your pressing chain, blunting sternal chest output and causing later Pec Deck sets to crash (as seen on Oct 19: 11 → 6 reps). Placing **Lateral Raises** between every chest movement gives your pecs and triceps 6–8 minutes of pure recovery while hammering your #1 priority muscle.
- **Dual-Profile Lateral Delt Stimulus**: **Cable Lateral Raise** loads the lengthened bottom range (stretch), while **DB Lateral Raise** loads the shortened top range (peak contraction + drop set). Total = **6 working sets + 1 drop set**.
- **Dynamic Warm-Up (3 Minutes Max)**: 15 arm circles forward/backward, 15 scapular wall slides, then **12.5 kg × 10 reps** + **17.5 kg × 4 feeler reps** on Incline DB Press before 22.5 kg.

### 2. Equipment & Progression Guardrails
- **NO 6.0 kg DB in Gym**: Rack jumps 5.0 kg → 7.0 kg (+40%). Stay strictly on **5.0 kg DBs** for DB Lateral Raises and push rep density (12–15+ reps with 2s eccentrics) + a Set 3 drop set to **2.5 kg**.
- **+5.0 kg Cable Stack Increments**: On Triceps Pushdown, master **12–15 clean reps across all 3 sets at 20.0 kg** before jumping +5 kg to the 25.0 kg pin.
- **RIR Standards**: Stop compounds (Incline DB, Smith Bench) at **1–2 RIR**. Push isolations (Cable/DB Lateral Raise, Pec Deck, Pushdowns) to **0–1 RIR**.

### 3. Gym Duration Guardrail: 50–55 Minutes Max
- **17 Working Sets Total**: Rest **150 sec** on Incline DB & Smith Bench, **60–90 sec** on Lateral Raises, Pec Deck, and Pushdowns. Use the app Rest Timer so sets stay tight.

## WORKOUT BLUEPRINT & EXERCISE TARGETS

### 1. Incline Dumbbell Bench Press (30° Bench)
- **Order Rationale**: **#1 Clavicular Upper-Chest Anchor.** Executed first while CNS and front delts are 100% fresh.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 6–10 (Set 1) / 8–12 (Sets 2–3) reps** | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
- **Target Weight**: **22.5 kg top-set / 20.0 kg back-off (per hand)**
  - *Warm-Up Ramp*: 12.5 kg × 10 reps, then **17.5 kg × 4 feeler reps** (critical neuromuscular primer).
  - *Main Target*: Set 1 @ **22.5 kg × 8–10 reps** (beat Nov 14's 6 reps); Sets 2–3 @ **20.0 kg × 10–12 reps**.
  - *Stretch Target*: 22.5 kg × 10 reps on Set 1 + 22.5 kg × 8–9 reps on Set 2 (matching Oct 19 PR).
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: If adjustable benches are full, start with **Smith Machine Bench Press** (#3) or use **Incline Machine Chest Press** (35.0–45.0 kg).
- **Form Cue**: Set bench strictly to **30° (second notch)**—never 45°. Tuck elbows **45°–60°** from torso, lower DBs to outer upper-chest stretch for 2 seconds, drive up smoothly without clacking weights at top.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Pin shoulder blades **down and back into the bench** before unracking. Think about sweeping your biceps inward across your collarbone shelf rather than pushing your hands away.

### 2. Cable Lateral Raise
- **Order Rationale**: **#2 Lengthened-Range Lateral Delt Priority & Active Chest Recovery.** Gives pecs/triceps 6–8 minutes of complete metabolic clearance before flat pressing while loading the medial delt under constant cable tension.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 60–90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
- **Target Weight**: **2.5 kg pin (Master 15 reps across all 3 sets before testing 5.0 kg)**
  - *Main Target*: 2.5 kg × 14, 13, 12+ reps per arm (matching/beating Nov 14's 3×12).
  - *Stretch Target*: 2.5 kg × 15, 15, 15 reps with strict 2-second eccentric return.
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: If cable stack is occupied, swap order with **Smith Machine Bench Press** (#3) or perform single-arm **Leaning DB Lateral Raise** (5.0 kg DB).
- **Form Cue**: Set pulley to **wrist/hip height**, cable crossing in front of thighs (or behind back). Stand tall, hold upright support lightly with non-working hand, sweep arm out in the scapular plane (15° forward) to shoulder height, and control a 2-second negative without letting the weight stack clank.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Depress your shoulder blade **down into your back pocket** before every rep. Think about dragging your **wrist/knuckles toward the opposite wall**, keeping your pinky level with your thumb.

### 3. Smith Machine Bench Press (Flat)
- **Order Rationale**: **#3 Refreshed Sternal Chest Mechanical Tension.** Executed after Cable Lateral Raises so pecs and triceps are cleared of incline-press fatigue.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 6–8 reps** | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
- **Target Weight**: **42.5 kg – 47.5 kg total (bar + plates)**
  - *Floor Target*: 42.5 kg × 8, 8, 8 reps.
  - *Main Target*: Set 1 @ **47.5 kg × 6–8 reps** (matching Oct 19 PR); Sets 2–3 @ **42.5 kg × 8–10 reps**.
  - *Stretch Target*: 47.5 kg × 8, 7, 6 reps across all 3 sets.
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: If Smith machine is taken, execute **Flat Barbell Bench Press** (40.0–42.5 kg) or **Seated Chest Press Machine** (40.0–45.0 kg) for 3 × 8–10 reps.
- **Form Cue**: Align bench so the bar touches your **lower sternum / nipple line** at the bottom. Plant feet flat into the floor, keep chest proud, and lower under a 2-second controlled descent without bouncing off the chest.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Imagine trying to **bend the bar inward** and squeeze your elbows together across your lower chest as you press. Keep shoulders depressed away from your ears.

### 4. Dumbbell Lateral Raise (+ Set 3 Drop Set)
- **Order Rationale**: **#4 Shortened-Range Lateral Delt Overload & Active Chest Breather.** Complements Cable Laterals by peaking tension at the top of the arc while resting chest before Pec Deck.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 12–15 reps (Set 3 drops immediately to 2.5 kg x 10–15)** | Rest: 60–90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
- **Target Weight**: **5.0 kg per hand (Drop set: 2.5 kg per hand)**
  - *Main Target*: 5.0 kg × 15, 13, 12 reps → immediate Drop Set: 2.5 kg × 12+ reps.
  - *Equipment Guardrail*: **NO 6.0 kg DB exists** and 7.0 kg (+40%) causes trap shrugging—earn overload via strict 2-second negatives and high-rep density at 5.0 kg.
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: If 5.0 kg DBs are taken, run a 4th–6th set on **Cable Lateral Raise** (2.5 kg) with a 1-second hold at peak abduction.
- **Form Cue**: Lean torso forward ~10–15°. Raise arms in the **scapular plane (15°–30° forward of your sides)**, stopping strictly at **collarbone/shoulder height**. Zero torso swing or knee bounce.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: **Pocket your shoulder blades down** before rep 1. Reach your **knuckles outward toward the far walls** (not upward toward the ceiling) and pull your elbows apart like stretching a tight elastic band.

### 5. Pec Deck Machine Fly
- **Order Rationale**: **#5 Pure Pectoral Isolation & Lengthened Stretch.** Chest is refreshed after DB Lateral Raises so you finish with strong squeeze instead of post-bench rep collapse.
- **Working Sets**: **2 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
- **Target Weight**: **35.0 kg pin (Fallback: 30.0 kg pin)**
  - *Main Target*: 35.0 kg × 12, 11–12 reps (matching and beating Nov 14's 12, 11).
  - *Stretch Target*: 35.0 kg × 12, 12 reps with a 1-second peak contraction pause on every rep.
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: If Pec Deck is busy, execute **Standing Cable Chest Fly** (set pulleys at shoulder height, 5.0–7.5 kg per side) for 2 × 12–15 reps.
- **Form Cue**: Set seat height so handles align directly with **mid-chest**. Keep elbows softly bent at a fixed angle (do not turn the fly into a press). Control a **3-second eccentric stretch** back until handles align with torso, then sweep smoothly inward.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Lead the inward sweep with your **inner elbows and wrists together**, not your hands. Hold a hard **1-second squeeze** when knuckles meet in front of your sternum.

### 6. Cable Triceps Pushdown (Straight Bar)
- **Order Rationale**: **#6 Lateral & Medial Triceps Lockout Finisher.** Carves the horseshoe triceps contour while elbows are thoroughly warmed up.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 60–90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
- **Target Weight**: **20.0 kg pin (No 22.5 kg pin; earn 3 × 12–15 before jumping to 25.0 kg)**
  - *Main Target*: 20.0 kg × 14, 13, 12 reps (beating Nov 14's 12, 12, 10).
  - *Stretch Target*: 20.0 kg × 15, 15, 13 reps to unlock the 25.0 kg pin next Push cycle.
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: If straight bar is occupied, use **Rope Triceps Pushdown** at 15.0–20.0 kg (spreading rope apart at bottom lockout) or **Overhead Cable Triceps Extension** at 20.0 kg.
- **Form Cue**: Stand tall with slight forward hip hinge, **pin upper arms/elbows strictly to your ribs**. Press bar smoothly down to **full elbow lockout**, hold 1 second, and control the return until forearms just clear parallel (90°–100°). Do NOT let elbows drift forward or flare out.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Think about pushing your **pinky-side palms straight into the floor** and flexing the outside horseshoe of your arm hard at every lockout.
