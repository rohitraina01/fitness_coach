## WORKOUT BLUEPRINT & EXERCISE TARGETS

### 1. Leg Press (Narrow / Mid-Low Stance)
- **Order Rationale**: **#1 Primary Quad Driver.** Maximum load tolerance without spinal compression or lower back strain.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
- **Target Weight**: **50.0 kg – 55.0 kg** (Oct 13: 45.0 kg × 12, 12, 12 conquered $\rightarrow$ Test 50.0 kg or 55.0 kg on Set 1!).
- **Warm-Up Ramp**: 25.0 kg × 10 reps, 35.0 kg × 6 reps before working sets.
- **Form Cue**: Position feet hip-width apart on the lower half of the sled. Keep lower back and pelvis glued firmly to the seat pad throughout the entire range of motion.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Lower sled until knees hit 90°. Do NOT bounce out of the bottom. Push through the mid-foot/heels and focus on driving your quads outward against the weight. Stop 2 inches short of soft lockout to maintain continuous mechanical tension on the vastus lateralis.

---

### 2. Leg Extension Machine
- **Order Rationale**: **#2 Pure Quad Isolation.** Hits the rectus femoris in full shortened contraction where leg press has zero tension.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
- **Target Weight**: **10.0 kg – 12.5 kg pin** (Oct 13: 10kg × 12 reps $\rightarrow$ Test 12.5 kg on Set 1, or solidify 10.0 kg for 15 strict reps).
- **Form Cue**: Align knee joints directly with the machine pivot axis. Pad sits comfortably on lower shins just above ankles.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Kick up smoothly. Lock out knees completely at the top and squeeze your quads hard for a strict 1-second hold. Lower the weight under control for 3 full seconds.

---

### 3. Lying or Seated Leg Curl Machine
- **Order Rationale**: **#3 Pure Hamstring Knee Flexion.** Strengthens hamstrings without hip extension (zero glute growth). Reverses Anterior Pelvic Tilt.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
- **Target Weight**: **5.0 kg – 7.5 kg pin** (Oct 13: 5.0 kg × 12, 10, 8 $\rightarrow$ Target: 5.0 kg × 12, 12, 12 or test 7.5 kg on Set 1).
- **Form Cue**: **DORSIFLEX FEET** (pull your toes up toward your shins). This simple biomechanical tweak prevents your gastrocnemius (calves) from assisting and forces 100% hamstring isolation.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Pull heels smoothly toward your glutes. Do NOT arch your lower back off the pad at the peak contraction. 3-second negative.

---

### 4. Standing Calf Raise (Machine or Dumbbell)
- **Order Rationale**: **#4 Lower Leg Athletic Proportions.** Creates the visual taper from knee to ankle.
- **Working Sets**: **4 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **0**
- **Target Weight**: **80.0 kg – 85.0 kg pin** (Oct 13: 80.0 kg × 12, 12, 12 $\rightarrow$ Advance to 85.0 kg on Set 1–2!).
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: Single-Leg Dumbbell Calf Raise on a step (15.0–20.0 kg DB in hand).
- **Form Cue**: Balls of feet on the edge of the block. Knees soft (slight 5° bend, not hyper-extended).
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Drop heels down into maximum deep stretch and **HOLD for 2 full seconds** (this kills the elastic Achilles tendon reflex). Drive up high on the balls of your big toes for a 1-second squeeze.

---

### 5. Bodyweight Floor Crunch (Anti-APT Abdominal Anchor)
- **Order Rationale**: **#5 Direct Rectus Abdominis Flexion.** Far superior to cable crunch; 100% mind-muscle core connection with zero hip flexor cheating.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
- **Target Weight**: **Bodyweight** (Controlled tempo).
- **Form Cue**: Lie flat on the floor, knees bent at 90°, feet flat. Press your lower back firmly into the floor so there is zero gap under your lumbar spine.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Do NOT pull on your neck. Imagine curling your ribcage down into your belt line. Squeeze your abs and exhale all air forcefully at the peak contraction.

---

### 6. Deadbugs (Deep Transverse Abdominis Activation)
- **Order Rationale**: **#6 Anterior Pelvic Tilt Reversal.** Directly counters 9 hours of seated desk posture.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 12–15 reps per side** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
- **Target Weight**: **Bodyweight**
- **Form Cue**: Lie on back, arms pointing to ceiling, hips and knees at 90°. Slowly extend opposite arm and leg toward the floor without letting your lower back arch off the floor.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Imagine bracing your core as if someone is about to step on your stomach. Only lower your leg as far as you can maintain 100% flat lower back contact with the floor.

---

### 7. Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch (Desk Worker Posture Reset)
- **Order Rationale**: **#7 Psoas Lengthening.** Directly releases shortened hip flexors from 9 hours of desk chair sitting.
- **Working Sets**: **2 sets x 45 seconds per side** | Rest: 30 sec
- **Execution**: Half-kneeling position (one knee down, one foot forward). Posterior pelvic tilt: tuck tailbone under and squeeze the glute of the trailing leg hard. Shift weight forward 2 inches until a deep stretch is felt in the front of the hip. Breathe deeply.
