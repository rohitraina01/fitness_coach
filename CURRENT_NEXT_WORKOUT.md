## COACHING BRIEFING & PROTOCOLS

### 1. Recommended Session Flow & Sequencing Rationale
Follow this 1 $\rightarrow$ 6 flow for maximum vastus lateralis quad sweep, hamstring knee flexion, diamond calves, and pelvic restoration:
- **Primary Quad Sweep Anchor**: **Leg Press (Narrow / Mid-Low Stance)** (3 sets × 10–12 reps @ 50.0–55.0 kg). Feet low-to-mid platform, hip-width apart. 100% quad loading; 0% glute engagement.
- **Pure Quad Isolation**: **Leg Extension Machine** (3 sets × 12–15 reps @ 10.0–12.0 kg pin). 1s hard peak lockout squeeze, 3s eccentric negative.
- **Hamstring Knee Flexion**: **Lying or Seated Leg Curl** (4 sets × 10–12 reps @ 5.0–7.5 kg pin). **Dorsiflex feet (pull toes hard toward shins)** to inhibit gastrocnemius assist and isolate pure hamstring knee flexion.
- **Calf Hypertrophy Anchor**: **Standing Calf Raise** (4 sets × 12–15 reps @ 80.0–85.0 kg pin). **2-second dead stop in the deep bottom stretch** to eliminate Achilles tendon elastic rebound.
- **Mandatory Anti-APT Core Reset**: **Floor Crunch & Deadbugs Superset** (3 sets × 12–15 crunches + 6–8 deadbugs per side). Lower back 100% glued flat to floor at all times.
- **Post-Workout Pelvic Reset**: **Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch** (2 sets × 45s per side) to lengthen shortened psoas from desk sitting.

### 2. Biomechanical Guardrails (0% Glute Work & APT Protection)
- **Zero Glute Recruitment Architecture**: Squats, deadlifts, hip thrusts, and lunges remain STRICTLY PROHIBITED. To build the aesthetic V-taper, hips and glutes must not widen. All lower-body volume targets the vastus lateralis (quad sweep) and hamstrings.
- **Leg Press Platform Stance**: Place feet **hip-width apart in the lower-middle half of the sled**. Do NOT place feet high or wide (which shifts loading to glutes and adductors). Stop descent before pelvis tilts or lumbar lifts off the seat ("butt wink").
- **Dorsiflexed Leg Curls**: When curling, actively pull toes upward toward your kneecap. If you point your toes (plantarflexion), calves take over 40% of the movement.
- **Core Lumbar Contact Rule**: On crunches and deadbugs, if the lower back lifts even 1 millimeter off the mat, stop the rep immediately. Arching means the hip flexors have hijacked the exercise.

### 3. Gym Duration Guardrail: 45–50 Minutes Max
- **Session Cap**: 4 lower-body movements (14 working sets) + 8-minute core finisher.
- Rest 120–150s on heavy Leg Press, 90s on Leg Extensions and Leg Curls, 60s on Calf Raises and Core. Execute with crisp tempo.

## WORKOUT BLUEPRINT & EXERCISE TARGETS

### 1. Leg Press (Narrow / Mid-Low Stance)
- **Order Rationale**: **#1 Vastus Lateralis Quad Mass Driver.** High stability compound pressing with zero spinal compression.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 120–150 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
- **Target Weight**: **50.0 kg – 55.0 kg sled**
  - *Floor Target*: 50.0 kg × 12, 12, 12 reps (solid baseline re-entry).
  - *Main Target*: 54.0 kg × 12, 12, 12 reps (matching Oct 21 all-time PR benchmark).
  - *Stretch Target*: 55.0 kg – 60.0 kg × 10–12 reps (overload leap into 60kg territory).
- **Warm-Up Ramp**: Sled only (or 20.0 kg) × 12 reps, 35.0 kg × 8 reps before working sets.
- **Form Cue**: Sit firmly back into seat with head against pad. Feet positioned hip-width apart on lower-to-middle half of platform. Lower the sled under strict control until knees reach 90°. Do NOT let lower back curl off the pad. Drive through mid-foot and heels; stop 1 inch short of knee lockout at top.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Feel the outside of your thighs (vastus lateralis) pushing the sled. Zero glute push.

---

### 2. Leg Extension Machine
- **Order Rationale**: **#2 Pure Rectus Femoris & Quad Isolation.** Isolates the quads in the shortened position where Leg Press falls off.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
- **Target Weight**: **10.0 kg – 12.0 kg pin**
  - *Floor Target*: 10.0 kg × 12, 12, 12 reps.
  - *Main Target*: Set 1 @ 12.0 kg (8–10 reps); Sets 2–3 @ 10.0 kg (12 reps).
  - *Stretch Target*: 12.0 kg × 12, 10, 10 reps across all 3 sets.
- **Form Cue**: Adjust back pad so knees align directly with machine pivot point. Shin pad rests comfortably against lower shins just above ankles. Grip side handles tightly to anchor hips into seat.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Kick up smoothly. At full extension, hold peak squeeze and flex quads for 1 full second. Lower under a strict 3-second eccentric return. Do NOT let weight stack clank between reps.

---

### 3. Lying or Seated Leg Curl Machine
- **Order Rationale**: **#3 Hamstring Knee Flexion & Pelvic Stabilizer.** Directly balances quadriceps pull on the pelvis to treat anterior tilt.
- **Working Sets**: **4 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
- **Target Weight**: **5.0 kg – 7.5 kg pin**
  - *Floor Target*: 5.0 kg × 12, 12, 10, 10 reps.
  - *Main Target*: Set 1 @ 7.5 kg (8–10 reps); Sets 2–4 @ 5.0 kg (12, 12, 10 reps).
  - *Stretch Target*: 7.5 kg × 10, 10, 8, 8 reps across all 4 sets.
- **Form Cue**: Lie face down with pad resting against lower calves (just below calves, above Achilles). **Pull toes hard toward shins (dorsiflexion)** and keep them pulled throughout the set. Grip handles to pin hips down flat against the bench.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Curl heels smoothly toward glutes using pure hamstring tension. Do NOT let your lower back hyperextend or hips rise off the bench. 2-second controlled descent.

### 4. Standing Calf Raise Machine
- **Order Rationale**: **#4 Lower Leg Aesthetics & Gastrocnemius Density.** Diamond calves frame the lower leg and balance the physique.
- **Working Sets**: **4 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **0**
- **Target Weight**: **80.0 kg – 85.0 kg pin**
  - *Floor Target*: 80.0 kg × 12, 12, 12, 12 reps.
  - *Main Target*: 80.0 kg × 15, 15, 12, 12 reps.
  - *Stretch Target*: 85.0 kg × 12, 12, 12, 10 reps.
- **Form Cue**: Balls of feet on block, heels hanging free. Legs straight with soft micro-bend in knees (do not lock out knees). Drop heels down into maximum possible stretch. **HOLD THE BOTTOM STRETCH FOR 2 FULL SECONDS (1-Mississippi, 2-Mississippi)** to kill tendon bounce. Explode onto big toes and hold peak contraction for 1 second.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Think about pushing the floor away with the balls of your big toes. Feel the deep stretch tear through the calf belly.

### 5. Anti-APT Core Finisher: Floor Crunch & Deadbugs Superset
- **Order Rationale**: **#5 Transverse Abdominis & Rectus Abdominis Restoration.** Pushes the pelvis backward into neutral alignment to erase desk posture belly protrusion.
- **Working Sets**: **3 supersets (12–15 crunches immediately into 6–8 deadbugs per side)** | Rest: 45–60 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
- **Target Weight**: **Bodyweight (0.0 kg)**
- **Floor Crunch Execution**:
  - Lie on back, knees bent 90°, feet flat on floor.
  - **Press lower back 100% flat into the mat (zero gap under lumbar spine)**.
  - Hands lightly touching temples. Curl your ribcage toward your pelvis; exhale every drop of air from your lungs at peak crunch. Hold peak contraction for 1 full second.
- **Deadbug Execution**:
  - Immediately raise knees to 90° tabletop position, arms pointing straight up toward ceiling.
  - Maintain absolute lumbar contact with the ground.
  - Slowly extend right arm overhead while extending left leg straight out hovering 2 inches off floor. Return and alternate sides.
  - *CRITICAL CUE*: If lower back arches off floor even 1mm, stop extension immediately. Quality over reach.

### 6. Post-Workout Posture Reset: Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch
- **Execution**: 2 sets × 45 seconds per side.
- **Form Cue**: Drop into a half-kneeling lunge on mat. Squeeze rear glute hard to force posterior pelvic tilt. Keep torso tall and tuck pelvis under. Do NOT arch lower back. Stretch must be felt deeply in the front hip crease (psoas) to undo 9 hours of seated desk posture.
