## COACHING BRIEFING & PROTOCOLS

Follow this 1 $\rightarrow$ 6 flow for maximum back thickness, V-taper lat width, and arm peak:
1. **Primer (Lat Wake-Up)**: **Standing Rope Straight-Arm Pulldown** (2 feeler sets × 12–15 reps). Shuts off the biceps and floods the lats with blood so you feel them contract immediately on your heavy compounds.
2. **Primary Vertical Lat Compound**: **Close-Grip / V-Bar Lat Pulldown** (3 sets × 8–10 reps @ 45–50 kg). Direct sagittal plane line of pull for the mid-to-lower lat fibers.
3. **Heavy Horizontal Density Anchor**: **Seated Cable Row (V-Bar)** (3 sets × 8–10 reps @ 60.0 kg Overload Leap!). Conquered 55kg × 12, 12, 12 on Oct 14—today we test 60kg!

5. **Posterior Delt Isolation**: **Reverse Pec Deck (Rear Delt Fly)** (3 sets × 10–12 reps @ 15.0 kg). Lock shoulder blades neutral to keep mid-traps out of it.
6. **Bicep Peak Overload**: **EZ-Bar Bicep Curl** (3 sets × 10–12 reps @ 20.0 kg). Strict form, elbows pinned.
7. **Arm Width & Forearms**: **Dumbbell Hammer Curl** (3 sets × 10–12 reps @ 7.5 kg DBs). Brachialis focus for 3D arm width.

## WORKOUT BLUEPRINT & EXERCISE TARGETS

### 0. Primer: Standing Rope Straight-Arm Pulldown (Lat Activation)
- **Order Rationale**: **#0 Lat Primer.** Neutralizes the biceps and establishes 10/10 mind-muscle connection before touching heavy weights.
- **Sets & Reps**: **2 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **2–3 (Feeler / zero failure)**
- **Target Weight**: **15.0 kg – 17.5 kg**
- **Form Cue**: Slight torso hinge at 15–20°, soft elbow bend locked in place. Pull rope down in a sweeping arc.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: At the bottom, flare your hands apart past your hips and squeeze your armpits into your sides for 1 full second.

---

### 1. Close-Grip / V-Bar Lat Pulldown
- **Order Rationale**: **#1 Primary Vertical Lat Driver.** Replaces bodyweight pull-ups to isolate pure lat fibers with locked-in thigh stability.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 8–10 reps** | Rest: 120 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
- **Target Weight**: **45.0 kg (Set 1 feeler/groove) $\rightarrow$ 50.0 kg (Sets 2 & 3 working load)**
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: Neutral-Grip Mag-Grip or Standard Wide Bar with moderate shoulder-width grip (45.0–50.0 kg).
- **Warm-Up Ramp**: 30.0 kg × 8 reps before working sets.
- **Form Cue**: Lock thighs tight under pads. Torso leaned back just 10–15°. Drive your elbows straight down into your ribs.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Think about bending your elbows into your back pockets. Do not jerk or heave backward.

---

### 2. Seated Cable Row (V-Bar / Close Grip)
- **Order Rationale**: **#2 Heavy Horizontal Back Density Anchor.** Primary mid-back and lower lat builder.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 8–10 reps** | Rest: 120 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
- **Target Weight**: **60.0 kg pin (OVERLOAD LEAP: 8–10 reps)**. *Fallback*: **55.0 kg pin** (10–12 reps if 60kg form breaks).
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: Chest-Supported T-Bar Row Machine (40.0–45.0 kg) or Chest-Supported Row Machine (45.0–50.0 kg).
- **Warm-Up Ramp**: 35.0 kg × 8 reps, 47.5 kg × 4 reps before working load.
- **Form Cue**: Torso upright at ~85° (minimal momentum). Full stretch forward, initiate pull by setting shoulder blades, then drive elbows backward.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Squeeze your mid-back for 1 second at peak contraction. Control the eccentric return for 2 seconds.

---

### 3. Single-Arm Cable Lat Row (or Single-Arm High-to-Low Pulldown)
- **Order Rationale**: **#3 Unilateral Lower Lat Sweep.** Matches your natural ribcage angle and fixes side-to-side imbalances.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 10–12 reps per side** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **1**
- **Target Weight**: **12.5 kg – 15.0 kg pin** (Completed 12.5kg × 12 on Oct 14).
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: 1-Arm Dumbbell Row with hand braced on bench (15.0–17.5 kg DB).
- **Form Cue**: Set cable pulley at knee-to-chest height. Sit or kneel. Hand in neutral grip.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Let the cable pull your arm forward for a deep lat stretch. Pull elbow back directly **into your hip crease**. Keep elbow tucked against your side.

---

### 4. Reverse Pec Deck (Rear Delt Fly)
- **Order Rationale**: **#4 Posterior Delt Hypertrophy & Posture Restoration.**
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
- **Target Weight**: **15.0 kg pin** (Oct 14: 15kg × 12, 12, 9 $\rightarrow$ Target today: 12, 12, 12!).
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: Cable Face Pulls with rope (15.0–17.5 kg) or Incline DB Rear Delt Flyes (4.0–5.0 kg DBs).
- **Form Cue**: Chest glued against pad, handles at shoulder height. Slight elbow bend locked.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Sweep arms wide toward room corners. Do not pinch shoulder blades together—isolate the rear delts.

---

### 5. EZ-Bar Bicep Curl
- **Order Rationale**: **#5 Direct Bicep Loading.** High mechanical tension for the biceps brachii.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **1**
- **Target Weight**: **20.0 kg** (Oct 14: 20kg × 15, 12, 11).
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: Incline DB Curls (7.5 kg DBs) or Standing Cable Curls (15.0–17.5 kg).
- **Form Cue**: Stand tall, elbows pinned to sides. Hinge strictly at the elbow joint without swinging hips.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Squeeze biceps at the top for 1 second, lower under 2-second control.

---

### 6. Dumbbell Hammer Curl
- **Order Rationale**: **#6 Brachialis & Forearm Width Finisher.**
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **0 (Failure on last set)**
- **Target Weight**: **7.5 kg DBs per hand** (Oct 14: 7.5kg × 10 reps).
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: Cable Rope Hammer Curls (17.5–20.0 kg pin).
- **Form Cue**: Neutral grip (thumbs up). Upper arms locked against ribs.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Squeeze forearms and outer biceps at the top. Strict 3-second negative.
