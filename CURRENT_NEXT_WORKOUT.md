## COACHING BRIEFING & PROTOCOLS

### 1. Recommended Session Flow & Sequencing Rationale
Follow this 1 $\rightarrow$ 6 flow for maximum back thickness, V-taper lat width, and arm peak:
1. **Pull-Up (Bodyweight or Assisted)**: Vertical pulling compound #1. Must be performed when central nervous system and grip are 100% fresh.
2. **Seated Cable Row (V-Bar or Neutral Close Grip)**: Heavy horizontal density anchor. Double-progression unlocked on Oct 14 (55kg × 12, 12, 12); today we test the **60.0 kg Overload Leap**!
3. **One-Arm Dumbbell Row**: Unilateral lat sweep. Eliminates side-to-side asymmetries and stretches lats deeply through full ROM.
4. **Reverse Pec Deck Machine (Rear Delt Fly) or Cable Face Pull**: Pure posterior delt isolation. Critical for 3D shoulder capping and pulling hunched desk shoulders back.
5. **Incline Dumbbell Curl**: Long-head bicep builder. Places biceps under extreme mechanical stretch behind the torso at a 45° incline.
6. **Dumbbell Hammer Curl (or Reverse Cable Curl)**: Brachialis and forearm thickness finisher to create massive upper arm width.

### 2. Equipment Leverage: Lifting Wrist Straps (Mandatory)
- **Deploy Straps on Movements #1, #2, and #3**: On Oct 14, lifting straps unlocked a 55kg row PR and eliminated forearm pump limits. Use straps on all heavy pulling sets today so your back fails before your fingers.

### 3. Dynamic Warm-Up Protocol (5–7 Mins)
- **Step 1: Scapular Dead Hangs & Retractions**: 10 slow reps (hang from bar, depress and pull shoulder blades down without bending elbows).
- **Step 2: Band Pull-Aparts**: 20 reps in the transverse plane, squeezing rear delts and rhomboids for 1 second.
- **Step 3: Rotator Cuff / Face Pull Feelers**: 15 reps with very light cable (10kg) to lubricate shoulder capsules.
- **Step 4: Seated Row Warm-Up Ramp**: 30.0 kg × 10 reps, 45.0 kg × 6 reps before loading 55kg/60kg working weight.

### 4. Mind-Muscle Connection (MMC) Master Cues

#### A. Horizontal Rows (Lat Sweep vs Upper Back)
- **The "Drive Elbows to Hip Crease" Cue**: Do not think about pulling the handle to your chest with your hands. Think about **driving your elbows backward into your rear pockets**.
- **Scapular Rhythm**: Allow shoulder blades to drift forward into a full stretch at the bottom; initiate the pull by pinching shoulder blades together *before* elbows bend.

#### B. Rear Delts (Zero Traps & Rhomboids)
- **Knuckles to Corners**: Reach your knuckles outward toward the room corners.
- **Do Not Pinch Scapulae**: Keep shoulder blades neutral/flared. If you pinch your shoulder blades together, your mid-traps and rhomboids hijack 80% of the rear delt tension.

#### C. Incline DB Curls (Biceps Long Head Stretch)
- **Elbows Behind Torso**: Set bench to 45–60°. Keep upper arms perpendicular to the floor, slightly behind torso.
- **Zero Shoulder Swiveling**: Hinge strictly at the elbow joint. Do not allow elbows to drift forward during the curl.

## WORKOUT BLUEPRINT & EXERCISE CUES

### 1. Pull-Up (Bodyweight or Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown)
- **Order Rationale**: **#1 Vertical Pulling Compound First.** Recruits maximum motor units when completely fresh.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 4–6 reps** (or 8–10 reps if using Lat Pulldown) | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
- **Target Weight**: **0.0 kg (Bodyweight: 4–6 strict reps) OR 50.0 kg Lat Pulldown (8–10 reps)**
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: If pull-up bar is occupied or shoulders feel stiff, perform Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown (Pin: 45.0–50.0 kg) or Assisted Pull-Up Machine (-20kg pin).
- **Warm-Up Ramp**: Scapular hang × 10 reps, 2 bodyweight feeler reps.
- **Form Cue**: Dead hang at the bottom. Depress scapulae down first, then drive elbows down to ribs. Pull upper chest up toward the bar.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Imagine bending the bar across your chest. Eliminate swinging or leg kicking.

---

### 2. Seated Cable Row (V-Bar or Neutral Close Grip)
- **Order Rationale**: **#2 Heavy Horizontal Back Density Anchor.** Primary mid-back and lower lat builder.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 8–10 reps** | Rest: 120 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
- **Target Weight**: **60.0 kg pin (OVERLOAD LEAP: 8–10 reps) OR 55.0 kg pin (Solidify: 10–12 reps with 1s pause)**
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: If low cable row is busy, use Chest-Supported T-Bar Row Machine (40.0–45.0 kg) or Chest-Supported Row Machine (45.0–50.0 kg).
- **Warm-Up Ramp**: 35.0 kg × 8 reps, 47.5 kg × 4 reps before working load.
- **Form Cue**: Torso stationary at ~85° upright (no excessive swinging). Wrap wrist straps tight around V-bar. Full stretch forward, drive elbows past ribs.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Squeeze shoulder blades together for 1 full second at full contraction. Let lats flare out wide during the 2-second negative return.

---

### 3. One-Arm Dumbbell Row
- **Order Rationale**: **#3 Unilateral Lat Sweep & Anti-Asymmetry.** Gives maximum lat stretch without spinal compression.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 8–10 reps per side** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
- **Target Weight**: **17.5 kg – 20.0 kg DB (Overload Leap: 8–10 reps)**. *Fallback*: **15.0 kg DB** (12 reps with 2s negative).
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: If dumbbells are occupied, use Single-Arm Low Cable Row (15.0–17.5 kg) or Chest-Supported DB Row (15.0 kg DBs).
- **Form Cue**: One knee and hand on flat bench (or standing staggered stance with one hand braced on dumbbell rack). Flat neutral spine.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Pull dumbbell in an arc toward your hip crease (not straight up into armpit). Keep elbow tucked tight against your side.

---

### 4. Reverse Pec Deck (Rear Delt Fly)
- **Order Rationale**: **#4 Posterior Delt Hypertrophy & Posture Restoration.** Balances heavy pressing and pulls shoulders back.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
- **Target Weight**: **15.0 kg pin (Solidify: 12, 12, 12 reps)**. *Fallback*: **12.5 kg pin** (15 reps with 2s contraction).
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: If machine is occupied, perform Cable Face Pulls with rope (15.0–17.5 kg) or Incline Prone DB Rear Delt Flyes (4.0–5.0 kg DBs).
- **Form Cue**: Chest glued against pad, seat adjusted so handles sit at shoulder height. Slight elbow bend locked in place.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Sweep arms outward in a wide circle. Reach knuckles toward room corners. Keep shoulder blades neutral—do not let mid-traps pinch.

---

### 5. Incline Dumbbell Curl
- **Order Rationale**: **#5 Long-Head Bicep Peak Isolation.** Extreme stretch profile for taller bicep peaks.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
- **Target Weight**: **7.5 kg DBs per hand (Primary: 10–12 reps)**. *Fallback*: **5.0 kg DBs** (12–15 reps with 3s eccentric).
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: If adjustable benches are taken, use Standing Cable Bicep Curls (15.0–17.5 kg) or Preacher Curl Bench with EZ Bar (15.0–17.5 kg).
- **Form Cue**: Bench inclined at 45–60°. Sit fully back, arms hanging straight down behind torso.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Supinate wrists (turn pinkies up) as you curl. Keep upper arms completely stationary; curl solely with the biceps without rocking shoulders forward.

---

### 6. Dumbbell Hammer Curl (or Cable Rope Hammer Curl)
- **Order Rationale**: **#6 Arm Thickness & Forearm Hypertrophy.** Recruits brachialis and brachioradialis for dense 3D arm width.
- **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **0**
- **Target Weight**: **7.5 kg DBs per hand (Primary: 10–12 reps)**. *Fallback*: **10.0 kg DBs** (6–8 reps) or **5.0 kg DBs** (15 slow reps).
- **Gym Floor Swap Option**: If dumbbells are busy, perform Cable Rope Hammer Curls (17.5–20.0 kg pin).
- **Form Cue**: Stand tall with neutral pelvis. Neutral grip (thumbs facing ceiling). Lock elbows against ribs.
- **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Squeeze forearms and outer arm hard at peak. Lower under strict 3-second control.
