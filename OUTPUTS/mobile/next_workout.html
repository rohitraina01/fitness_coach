DAY 3 - LEGS & CORE (QUAD SWEEP, CALVES & ANTI-APT POSTURE)

Evidence-Based Hypertrophy • 5-Day Aesthetic Split
🧠 Coach Protocols & Warm-Up ▼

1. Recommended Session Flow & Sequencing Rationale

  • Follow this 1 $\rightarrow$ 6 flow for athletic quad sweep, diamond calves, and 9-hour desk Anterior Pelvic Tilt (APT) reversal:
  • 1. **Primary Quad Mass Anchor**: **Leg Press (Narrow / Mid-Low Foot Stance)** (3 working sets × 10–12 reps @ 50.0–55.0 kg). Feet low and narrow on the platform. Direct quad recruitment; 0% glute recruitment. Oct 13 conquered 45kg for 12, 12, 12 $\rightarrow$ Today we test 50–55 kg!
  • 2. **Pure Quad Isolation (Peak Extension)**: **Leg Extension Machine** (3 sets × 12–15 reps @ 10.0–12.5 kg). 1-second pause at full knee lockout, 3-second descent. Eliminates hip flexor and glute involvement completely.
  • 3. **Hamstring Knee-Flexion Specialist**: **Lying or Seated Leg Curl** (3 sets × 10–12 reps @ 5.0–7.5 kg). Feet dorsiflexed (toes pulled to shins). Strengthens hamstrings to pull the anterior-tilted pelvis back to neutral.
  • 4. **Calf Thickness & Athletic Taper**: **Standing Calf Raise** (4 sets × 12–15 reps @ 80.0–85.0 kg). 2-second dead stop in the deep stretch at the bottom to kill tendon elastic bounce.
  • 5. **Anti-APT Core Anchor**: **Bodyweight Floor Crunch** (3 sets × 12–15 reps). Lower back glued 100% flat into the floor. Curl ribcage toward pelvis; exhale all air at top.
  • 6. **Deep Core Transverse Abdominis Activation**: **Deadbugs** (3 sets × 12–15 reps). Opposite arm/leg reach with zero lumbar arch.
  • 7. **Post-Workout 9-Hour Desk Psoas Release**: **Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch** (2 sets × 45s/side). Posterior pelvic tilt tuck; squeeze rear glute hard.

2. Biomechanical Guardrails (Strict Anti-Glute & Anti-APT Rules)

  • **ZERO Barbell Squats, Deadlifts, or Lunges**: Strictly prohibited. We want quad sweep and V-taper frame, NOT wider hips or larger glutes.
  • **Narrow Foot Placement on Leg Press**: Never place feet high or wide on the sled (which shifts load to glutes). Keep feet hip-width, mid-low on platform.
  • **Lower Back Floor Contact**: On all abdominal work, if your lower back leaves the floor by even 1 millimeter, stop immediately. The psoas took over.

3. Gym Duration Guardrail: 50–55 Minutes Max

  • Move directly between stations. Rest 150s on Leg Press, 90s on Leg Extension/Curl, 60s on Calves and Core.
#1 1. Leg Press (Narrow / Mid-Low Stance)
**3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
🎯 **50.0 kg – 55.0 kg** (Oct 13: 45.0 kg × 12, 12, 12 conquered $\rightarrow$ Test 50.0 kg or 55.0 kg on Set 1!).

Position feet hip-width apart on the lower half of the sled. Keep lower back and pelvis glued firmly to the seat pad throughout the entire range of motion.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#1 Primary Quad Driver.** Maximum load tolerance without spinal compression or lower back strain.

Lower sled until knees hit 90°. Do NOT bounce out of the bottom. Push through the mid-foot/heels and focus on driving your quads outward against the weight. Stop 2 inches short of soft lockout to maintain continuous mechanical tension on the vastus lateralis.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **50.0 kg – 55.0 kg** (Oct 13: 45.0 kg × 12, 12, 12 conquered $\rightarrow$ Test 50.0 kg or 55.0 kg on Set 1!).

#2 2. Leg Extension Machine
**3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
🎯 **10.0 kg – 12.5 kg pin** (Oct 13: 10kg × 12 reps $\rightarrow$ Test 12.5 kg on Set 1, or solidify 10.0 kg for 15 strict reps).

Align knee joints directly with the machine pivot axis. Pad sits comfortably on lower shins just above ankles.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#2 Pure Quad Isolation.** Hits the rectus femoris in full shortened contraction where leg press has zero tension.

Kick up smoothly. Lock out knees completely at the top and squeeze your quads hard for a strict 1-second hold. Lower the weight under control for 3 full seconds.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **10.0 kg – 12.5 kg pin** (Oct 13: 10kg × 12 reps $\rightarrow$ Test 12.5 kg on Set 1, or solidify 10.0 kg for 15 strict reps).

#3 3. Lying or Seated Leg Curl Machine
**3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
🎯 **5.0 kg – 7.5 kg pin** (Oct 13: 5.0 kg × 12, 10, 8 $\rightarrow$ Target: 5.0 kg × 12, 12, 12 or test 7.5 kg on Set 1).

**DORSIFLEX FEET** (pull your toes up toward your shins). This simple biomechanical tweak prevents your gastrocnemius (calves) from assisting and forces 100% hamstring isolation.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#3 Pure Hamstring Knee Flexion.** Strengthens hamstrings without hip extension (zero glute growth). Reverses Anterior Pelvic Tilt.

Pull heels smoothly toward your glutes. Do NOT arch your lower back off the pad at the peak contraction. 3-second negative.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **5.0 kg – 7.5 kg pin** (Oct 13: 5.0 kg × 12, 10, 8 $\rightarrow$ Target: 5.0 kg × 12, 12, 12 or test 7.5 kg on Set 1).

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **80.0 kg – 85.0 kg pin** (Oct 13: 80.0 kg × 12, 12, 12 $\rightarrow$ Advance to 85.0 kg on Set 1–2!).

#5 5. Bodyweight Floor Crunch (Anti-APT Abdominal Anchor)
**3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
🎯 **Bodyweight** (Controlled tempo).

Lie flat on the floor, knees bent at 90°, feet flat. Press your lower back firmly into the floor so there is zero gap under your lumbar spine.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#5 Direct Rectus Abdominis Flexion.** Far superior to cable crunch; 100% mind-muscle core connection with zero hip flexor cheating.

Do NOT pull on your neck. Imagine curling your ribcage down into your belt line. Squeeze your abs and exhale all air forcefully at the peak contraction.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **Bodyweight** (Controlled tempo).

#6 6. Deadbugs (Deep Transverse Abdominis Activation)
**3 sets x 12–15 reps per side** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
🎯 **Bodyweight**

Lie on back, arms pointing to ceiling, hips and knees at 90°. Slowly extend opposite arm and leg toward the floor without letting your lower back arch off the floor.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#6 Anterior Pelvic Tilt Reversal.** Directly counters 9 hours of seated desk posture.

Imagine bracing your core as if someone is about to step on your stomach. Only lower your leg as far as you can maintain 100% flat lower back contact with the floor.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **Bodyweight**
