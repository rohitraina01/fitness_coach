DAY 1 - PUSH A (CYCLE 3 • UPPER CHEST, HEAVY DELTS & TRICEPS)

Rule 9 satisfied via Nov 27 Legs & Core (Leg Extension 12kg PR & 54kg Leg Press matched). Chest, anterior delts, and triceps are 100% rested (14 days since Nov 14 Push B) and primed for upper-chest & shoulder overload.
🧠 Coach Protocols & Warm-Up ▼

1. Recommended Session Flow & Warm-Up Protocol

  • Dynamic Warm-Up (3 Minutes Max): 15 band/arm circles forward & backward, 15 scapular wall slides, and 2 ramp-up sets on Incline DB Press (12.5 kg × 10 reps + 17.5 kg × 4 feeler reps). The 17.5 kg feeler set is mandatory so your nervous system is primed before touching 22.5 kg!
  • Sequence 1 $\rightarrow$ 5:
  • 1. Incline Dumbbell Press (30°): Heavy clavicular upper-chest anchor while CNS is 100% fresh.
  • 2. Smith Machine Bench Press: Fixed-path sternal chest mechanical tension (no barbell wobble).
  • 3. Dumbbell Lateral Raise: High-volume shoulder capping (4 sets + Set 4 drop set).
  • 4. Pec Deck Machine Fly: Deep pectoral stretch and 1-second apex squeeze.
  • 5. Cable Triceps Pushdown: Lateral/medial horseshoe triceps lockout to finish.

2. Equipment & Progression Guardrails

  • NO 6.0 kg DB in Gym: Rack jumps 5.0 kg $\rightarrow$ 7.0 kg (+40%). Stay strictly on 5.0 kg DBs for Lateral Raises and push rep density (12–15+ reps with 2s eccentrics) + a Set 4 drop set to 2.5 kg. Jumping to 7.0 kg forces upper-trap shrugging.
  • +5.0 kg Cable Stack Increments: On Triceps Pushdown, master 12–15 clean reps across all 3 sets at 20.0 kg before jumping +5 kg to the 25.0 kg pin.
  • RIR Standards: Stop compounds (Incline DB, Smith Bench) at 1–2 RIR (clean bar speed, zero shoulder roll). Push isolations (Lateral Raise, Pec Deck, Pushdowns) to 0–1 RIR.

3. Gym Duration Guardrail: 50–55 Minutes Max

  • 15 Working Sets Total: Rest 150 sec on Incline DB & Smith Bench, 90 sec on Lateral Raises & Pec Deck, 60–90 sec on Triceps Pushdowns. Use the app Rest Timer so sets don't drift.
#1 1. Incline Dumbbell Bench Press (30° Bench)
3 sets x 6–10 (Set 1) / 8–12 (Sets 2–3) reps | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: 1–2
🎯 22.5 kg top-set / 20.0 kg back-off (per hand)

Set bench strictly to 30° (second notch)—never 45°, which shifts tension to front delts. Tuck elbows 45°–60° from torso, lower DBs to outer upper-chest stretch for 2 seconds, drive up smoothly without clacking weights at top.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: #1 Clavicular Upper-Chest Anchor. Executed first while pressing CNS and front delts are 100% fresh.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If all adjustable benches are occupied, use Incline Machine Chest Press (35.0–45.0 kg) or start with Smith Machine Bench Press (#2) and return to Incline DBs.

Pin shoulder blades down and back into the bench before unracking. Think about pushing your biceps inward across your collarbone shelf rather than pushing your hands away.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: 22.5 kg top-set / 20.0 kg back-off (per hand)

#2 2. Smith Machine Bench Press (Flat)
3 sets x 6–8 reps | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: 1–2
🎯 42.5 kg – 47.5 kg total (bar + plates)

Align bench so the bar touches your lower sternum / nipple line at the bottom. Plant feet flat into the floor, keep chest proud, and lower under a 2-second controlled descent without bouncing off the chest.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: #2 Fixed-Path Sternal Chest Mass Driver. High mechanical tension without stabilizer fatigue after heavy incline DBs.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If the Smith machine is taken, execute Flat Barbell Bench Press (40.0–42.5 kg) or Seated Chest Press Machine (40.0–45.0 kg) for 3 × 8–10 reps.

Imagine trying to bend the bar inward and squeeze your elbows together across your lower chest as you press. Keep shoulders depressed away from your ears.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: 42.5 kg – 47.5 kg total (bar + plates)

#3 3. Dumbbell Lateral Raise (+ Set 4 Drop Set)
4 sets x 12–15 reps (Set 4 drops immediately to 2.5 kg x 10–15) | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
🎯 5.0 kg per hand (Drop set: 2.5 kg per hand)

Lean torso forward ~10–15°. Raise arms in the scapular plane (15°–30° forward of your sides), stopping strictly at collarbone/shoulder height. Zero torso swing or knee bounce.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: #3 Top-Priority Lateral Delt Width Builder. Hits the medial delts before triceps/chest fatigue ruins posture.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If 5.0 kg DBs are missing, use Cable Lateral Raise at 2.5 kg pin (pulley at wrist height) for 4 × 12–15 reps per arm.

Pocket your shoulder blades down before rep 1. Reach your knuckles outward toward the far walls (not upward toward the ceiling) and pull your elbows apart like stretching a tight elastic band.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: 5.0 kg per hand (Drop set: 2.5 kg per hand)

#4 4. Pec Deck Machine Fly
2 sets x 10–12 reps | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
🎯 35.0 kg pin (Fallback: 30.0 kg pin)

Set seat height so handles align directly with mid-chest. Keep elbows softly bent at a fixed angle (do not turn the fly into a press). Control a 3-second eccentric stretch back until handles align with torso, then sweep smoothly inward.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: #4 Pure Pectoral Isolation & Lengthened Stretch. Strips triceps completely out of pressing to finish chest fibers cleanly.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If Pec Deck is busy, execute Standing Cable Chest Fly (set pulleys at shoulder height, 5.0–7.5 kg per side) for 2 × 12–15 reps.

Lead the inward sweep with your inner elbows and wrists together, not your hands. Hold a hard 1-second squeeze when knuckles meet in front of your sternum.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: 35.0 kg pin (Fallback: 30.0 kg pin)

#5 5. Cable Triceps Pushdown (Straight Bar)
3 sets x 12–15 reps | Rest: 60–90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
🎯 20.0 kg pin (No 22.5 kg pin; earn 3 × 12–15 before jumping to 25.0 kg)

Stand tall with slight forward hip hinge, pin upper arms/elbows strictly to your ribs. Press bar smoothly down to full elbow lockout, hold 1 second, and control the return until forearms just clear parallel (90°–100°). Do NOT let elbows drift forward or flare out.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: #5 Lateral & Medial Triceps Lockout Finisher. Carves the horseshoe triceps contour while elbows are thoroughly warmed up.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If straight bar is occupied, use Rope Triceps Pushdown at 15.0–20.0 kg (spreading rope apart at bottom lockout) or Overhead Cable Triceps Extension at 20.0 kg.

Think about pushing your pinky-side palms straight into the floor and flexing the outside horseshoe of your arm hard at every lockout.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: 20.0 kg pin (No 22.5 kg pin; earn 3 × 12–15 before jumping to 25.0 kg)
