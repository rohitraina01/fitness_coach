DAY 7 - PULL B (HIGH-STABILITY BACK DENSITY, REAR DELTS & ARMS + ANTI-APT CORE)

Evidence-Based Hypertrophy • 5-Day Aesthetic Split
🧠 Coach Protocols & Warm-Up ▼

1. Recommended Session Flow & Sequencing Rationale

  • Follow this 1 $\rightarrow$ 6 flow for maximum lat width, horizontal back density, arm thickness, and posture restoration:
  • **Vertical Sagittal Lat Driver**: **Close-Grip / V-Bar Lat Pulldown** (3 sets × 8–10 reps @ 45.0–50.0 kg). Thigh pads locked down tight. Straps on. Drive elbows down into back pockets.
  • **High-Stability Horizontal Thickness**: **Chest-Supported Machine Row or Seated Cable Row** (3 sets × 8–10 reps @ 60.0–65.0 kg). Sternum glued flat to pad or upright torso locked dead still. Squeeze rhomboids at apex for 1s.
  • **Unilateral Bench-Braced Lats**: **Bench-Braced One-Arm DB Row** (3 sets × 8–10 reps @ 17.5–20.0 kg DB). Knee and hand planted on flat bench. Zero spinal rotation or hip twisting.
  • **Rear Delt Capping**: **Reverse Pec Deck (Rear Delt Fly)** (3 sets × 12–15 reps @ 15.0–17.5 kg pin). Sternum against pad. Sweep knuckles wide toward side walls.
  • **Arm Hypertrophy Superset**: **EZ-Bar Curl + DB Hammer Curl** (3 supersets × 10–12 reps @ 20.0 kg bar + 7.0–8.0 kg DBs). Strict elbow lock to ribs; 3s eccentric descent.
  • **Mandatory Anti-APT Core Reset**: **Floor Crunch & Deadbugs Superset** (3 sets × 12–15 crunches + 6–8 deadbugs/side). Lower back 100% glued flat to floor. Finish with 45s/side Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch to lengthen tight psoas from yesterday's trek.

2. Biomechanical Guardrails (Post-Trek Lumbar & Spinal Protection)

  • **Zero Axial / Spinal Loading**: Because yesterday's trek loaded the lumbar spine and hip stabilizers, freestanding barbell rows and wobbly cable rows are STRICTLY PROHIBITED today. Every pulling movement must feature chest support or bench bracing.
  • **Lifting Wrist Straps Mandatory**: Forearms and grip were heavily taxed on the trek. Eliminate forearm failure from back training by locking straps on all pulldown and row sets.
  • **Anti-APT Pelvic Reset is Non-Negotiable**: Trekking shortens the hip flexors (psoas). You skipped quads today, but you CANNOT skip the core and hip flexor stretch. Press lumbar flat into the floor on crunches and deadbugs.
  • **Next Session Hard-Locked**: 3 consecutive upper body workouts have now occurred (Pull A Nov 7, Push B Nov 14, Pull B today). The next session is 100% LOCKED to Legs & Core. Zero pressing until leg volume is logged.

3. Gym Duration Guardrail: 45–50 Minutes Max

  • **Hard Session Cap**: 5 lifting movements (14 working sets) + 8-minute core finisher.
  • Rest 120s on compound pulldowns/rows, 90s on DB rows, 60s on rear delts, arms, and core. Execute with surgical pace.
#1 1. Close-Grip / V-Bar Lat Pulldown
**3 sets x 8–10 reps** | Rest: 120 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
🎯 **45.0 kg – 50.0 kg pin**

Thigh pad locked firmly against quads so hips cannot rise. Lean back only 10–15° (no swinging). Drive elbows straight down toward your hips. Full controlled stretch at the top without letting shoulders shrug to ears.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#1 Vertical Sagittal Lat Mass Driver.** Recruits lower lats through shoulder extension with zero spinal torque while fresh.

Pretend your hands are passive hooks. Pull entirely through the tips of your elbows. Squeeze lower lats hard at bottom chest touch.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **45.0 kg – 50.0 kg pin**

#2 2. Chest-Supported Machine Row (or Seated Cable Row)
**3 sets x 8–10 reps** | Rest: 120 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
🎯 **60.0 kg – 65.0 kg pin**

Sternum glued flat against chest pad (or if using Seated Cable Row, sit tall with chest proud and spine dead-stop stationary). Pull handles toward lower abdomen.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#2 Horizontal Mid-Back & Rhomboid Thickness.** Sternum support eliminates lumbar fatigue from yesterday's trek.

Retract shoulder blades first, then drive elbows backward past your ribcage. Hold peak squeeze for 1 full second to force rhomboid and mid-trap thickness. 2-second negative return.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **60.0 kg – 65.0 kg pin**

#3 3. Bench-Braced One-Arm DB Row
**3 sets x 8–10 reps per arm** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
🎯 **17.5 kg – 20.0 kg DB**

Place left knee and left hand firmly on a flat bench. Right foot braced wide on floor. Back flat and parallel to floor. Grip dumbbell in right hand.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#3 Unilateral Lat Width & Stability Anchor.** Three-point bench contact provides 100% torso rigidity without lower back shear.

Pull the dumbbell smoothly in an arcing path toward your hip pocket, not up into your armpit. Feel the lat stretch at the bottom without letting your torso rotate.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **17.5 kg – 20.0 kg DB**

#4 4. Reverse Pec Deck (Rear Delt Fly)
**3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
🎯 **15.0 kg – 17.5 kg pin**

Seat height adjusted so handles align with mid-chest. Chest pressed against pad. Neutral grip (palms facing inward). Slight micro-bend in elbows.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#4 Posterior Delt 3D Hypertrophy.** Balances anterior delt dominance and builds 3D capped shoulder appearance.

Sweep knuckles outward toward the side walls. Do NOT pinch shoulder blades together—keep scapulae protracted so the posterior delts do 100% of the work.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **15.0 kg – 17.5 kg pin**

#5 5. EZ-Bar Bicep Curl & Dumbbell Hammer Curl (Arm Hypertrophy Superset)
**3 supersets (10–12 EZ curls immediately into 10–12 hammer curls)** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
🎯 **20.0 kg EZ-Bar + 7.0 kg – 8.0 kg DBs**

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#5 Bicep Peak & Brachialis Thickness.** Superset compound elbow flexors to blow up arm diameter in minimal time.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **20.0 kg EZ-Bar + 7.0 kg – 8.0 kg DBs**

**3 supersets (12–15 crunches immediately into 6–8 deadbugs per side)** | Rest: 45 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
🎯 **Bodyweight (0.0 kg)**

#7 7. Post-Workout Posture Reset: Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch
3 sets x 8–12 reps

Drop into half-kneeling lunge on mat. Squeeze rear glute hard to force posterior pelvic tilt. Keep torso tall and tuck pelvis under. Do NOT arch lower back. Stretch must be felt deeply in the front hip crease (psoas) to undo trekking tightness.
