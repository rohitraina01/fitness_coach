DAY 2 - PULL A (LAT WIDTH & BICEP HYPERTROPHY)

Evidence-Based Hypertrophy • 5-Day Aesthetic Split
🧠 Coach Protocols & Warm-Up ▼

1. Recommended Session Flow & Sequencing Rationale

  • Follow this 1 $\rightarrow$ 6 flow for maximum lat width, upper back thickness, and 3D bicep peaks:
  • 1. **Vertical Lat Width Anchor**: **Lat Pulldown (Neutral or Moderate Grip)** (3 sets × 8–10 reps @ 45.0–50.0 kg). Drive elbows straight down into ribcage pockets. Deploy wrist straps to lock grip.
  • 2. **Horizontal Upper Back Scapular Retraction**: **Chest-Supported Row Machine** (3 sets × 8–10 reps @ 40.0–45.0 kg). Chest glued to pad; eliminate torso sway. Squeeze shoulder blades together for 1s.
  • 3. **Pure Lat Isolation / MMC Stretch**: **Straight-Arm Cable Pulldown** (3 sets × 12–15 reps @ 15.0 kg). High motor-unit recruitment with soft elbows. Sweep bar/rope down into upper thighs.
  • 4. **Rear Delt Fly & Scapular Decoupling**: **Reverse Pec Deck (Rear Delt Fly)** (3 sets × 12–15 reps @ 15.0–20.0 kg). Neutral handles, sweep knuckles outward to room corners.
  • 5. **Bicep Peak & Mechanical Tension**: **EZ-Bar Bicep Curl** (3 sets × 10–12 reps @ 20.0 kg). Strict form with elbows pinned to ribs. No hip swinging.
  • 6. **Brachialis & Forearm Thickness**: **Dumbbell Hammer Curl** (3 sets × 10–12 reps @ 7.0–8.0 kg DBs). Neutral grip. Test 8.0 kg DBs on Set 1 or dominate 7.0 kg with controlled eccentrics.

2. Gym Equipment Reality Rules (Applied to Today's Session)

  • **Machine Stacks (+5 kg Steps)**: For cable pulldowns and rows, plates transition to +5 kg increments. On isolations (Straight-Arm Pulldowns, Rear Delts), do NOT jump prematurely if +5 kg degrades form; accumulate reps to 15 first.
  • **Dumbbell Rack (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8 kg)**: There is NO 6.0 kg DB. For Hammer Curls, transition from 7.0 kg directly to 8.0 kg DBs per hand.
  • **Lifting Wrist Straps**: **MANDATORY** on Lat Pulldowns and Chest-Supported Rows. Completely eliminates forearm/grip fatigue so lats take 100% of the load.

3. Gym Duration Guardrail: 55–60 Minutes Max

  • **Zero Marathon Sessions**: Oct 19 was 75m due to talking. Today, keep your phone in your pocket, use the built-in rest timer (120–150s compounds, 90s isolations), and execute in under 60 minutes.

4. Dynamic Warm-Up Protocol (5 Mins)

  • **Dead Hang from Pull-Up Bar**: 2 × 30 seconds (Decompresses lumbar spine from 9h desk sitting and activates scapular depressors).
  • **Band Pull-Aparts**: 20 slow reps with 1s squeeze to prime lower traps and rhomboids.
  • **Lat Pulldown Feeler**: 1 light set of 10 reps @ 25.0 kg to groove vertical lat pull line.
