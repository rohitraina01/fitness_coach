DAY 3 - LEGS & POSTURE (QUAD SWEEP, CALVES & ANTI-PELVIC TILT)

Evidence-Based Hypertrophy • 5-Day Aesthetic Split
#1 1. Leg Press (Narrow / Mid-Low Stance)
  • **Warm-Up**: 2 sets (e.g. empty sled x 12, +40kg x 8).
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: 1–2
  • **Targets**:
  • **Floor**: 3 sets x 10 reps (Establish initial working baseline).
  • **Main**: 3 sets x 10–12 reps with standardized 90° knee flexion.
  • **Stretch**: 3 sets x 12 reps with locked torso and slow eccentric.
  • **Form Cue**: Feet placed hip-width apart and low-to-mid platform to maximize quad sweep. Never let your lower back or pelvis peel off the back pad at the bottom.
#2 2. Leg Extension Machine
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
  • **Targets**:
  • **Floor**: 3 sets x 12 reps.
  • **Main**: 3 sets x 13–15 reps with distinct 1-second squeeze at full contraction.
  • **Stretch**: 3 sets x 15 reps (triggers load increase next session).
  • **Form Cue**: Pure quad isolation (0% glute recruitment). Hold peak lockout for 1 full second, then take 3 seconds to lower the weight smoothly.
#3 3. Seated or Lying Leg Curl
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
  • **Targets**:
  • **Floor**: 3 sets x 10 reps.
  • **Main**: 3 sets x 11–12 reps with controlled tempo.
  • **Stretch**: 3 sets x 12 reps.
  • **Form Cue**: Pure knee flexion (zero glute involvement). Pull heels firmly toward glutes, pause briefly, and control the eccentric stretch to restore hamstring length.
#4 4. Standing Calf Raise
  • **Working Sets**: **4 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: 0
  • **Targets**:
  • **Floor**: 4 sets x 12 reps.
  • **Main**: 4 sets x 12–15 reps with dead-stop stretch.
  • **Stretch**: 4 sets x 15 reps.
  • **Form Cue**: Full range of motion. Pause for 2 full seconds in the deep bottom stretch on every single rep to eliminate Achilles tendon bounce before driving up onto the big toes.
#5 5. Deadbugs or Hanging Knee Raise
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
  • **Targets**:
  • **Floor**: 3 sets x 12 reps.
  • **Main**: 3 sets x 15 reps with strict abdominal bracing.
  • **Stretch**: 3 sets x 15 reps with 2s hold in peak contraction.
  • **Form Cue**: Mandatory Anti-APT movement. Glue lower back 100% flat against the floor or pad. Pull pelvis into a posterior tilt and brace the core like preparing for a punch.
#6 6. Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch
  • **Working Sets**: **2 sets x 45–60 sec per side** | Rest: 30 sec | Target RIR: —
  • **Targets**:
  • **Main**: 2 sets x 45–60 seconds per leg.
  • **Form Cue**: Essential for desk workers. Drop rear knee to mat, tuck pelvis under into a posterior tilt, and squeeze the trailing glute forward to release the chronically tight psoas from 9 hours of sitting.
