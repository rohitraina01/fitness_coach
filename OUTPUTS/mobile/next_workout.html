#1 1. Incline Dumbbell Bench Press (30° Bench)
3 sets x 6–10 (Set 1) / 8–12 (Sets 2–3) reps | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: 1–2
🎯 22.5 kg top-set / 20.0 kg back-off (per hand)

Set bench strictly to 30° (second notch)—never 45°. Tuck elbows 45°–60° from torso, lower DBs to outer upper-chest stretch for 2 seconds, drive up smoothly without clacking weights at top.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: #1 Clavicular Upper-Chest Anchor. Executed first while CNS and front delts are 100% fresh after 14 days rest.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If adjustable benches are full, start with Smith Machine Bench Press (#3) or use Incline Machine Chest Press (35.0–45.0 kg).

Pin shoulder blades down and back into the bench before unracking. Think about sweeping your biceps inward across your collarbone shelf rather than pushing your hands away.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: 22.5 kg top-set / 20.0 kg back-off (per hand)

#2 2. Cable Lateral Raise
3 sets x 12–15 reps | Rest: 60–90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
🎯 2.5 kg pin (Master 3 × 15 before jumping to 5.0 kg)

Set pulley to wrist/hip height, cable crossing in front of thighs (or behind back). Stand tall, hold upright support lightly with non-working hand, sweep arm out in the scapular plane (15° forward) to shoulder height, and control a 2-second negative without letting the weight stack clank.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: #2 Lengthened-Range Lateral Delt Priority & Active Chest Recovery. Gives pecs/triceps 6–8 minutes of metabolic clearance before Smith Bench while loading the medial delt in the bottom stretch.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If cable stack is occupied, swap order with Smith Machine Bench Press (#3) or perform single-arm Leaning DB Lateral Raise (5.0 kg DB).

Depress your shoulder blade down into your back pocket before every rep. Think about dragging your wrist/knuckles toward the opposite wall, keeping your pinky level with your thumb.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: 2.5 kg pin (Master 3 × 15 before jumping to 5.0 kg)

#3 3. Smith Machine Bench Press (Flat)
2 sets x 6–8 reps | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: 1–2
🎯 42.5 kg – 47.5 kg total (bar + plates)

Align bench so the bar touches your lower sternum / nipple line at the bottom. Plant feet flat into the floor, keep chest proud, and lower under a 2-second controlled descent without bouncing off the chest.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: #3 Refreshed Sternal Chest Mechanical Tension. Re-establishes flat pressing strength after a 40-day layoff while pecs and triceps are cleared of incline-press fatigue.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If Smith machine is taken, execute Flat Barbell Bench Press (37.5–42.5 kg) or Seated Chest Press Machine (40.0–45.0 kg) for 2 × 8–10 reps.

Imagine trying to bend the bar inward and squeeze your elbows together across your lower chest as you press. Keep shoulders depressed away from your ears.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: 42.5 kg – 47.5 kg total (bar + plates)

#4 4. Overhead Cable Triceps Extension
3 sets x 8–12 reps | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
🎯 20.0 kg pin (Master 12+ reps before +5 kg jump to 25.0 kg)

Set cable at chest/shoulder height, face away in a staggered split stance, lean torso slightly forward. Keep upper arms angled overhead and freeze elbows in space—allow forearms to fold all the way behind your head for a 1-second deep stretch before extending cleanly.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: #4 Stretch-Mediated Long-Head Triceps Priority. Placed before Pec Deck so chest recovers completely while you drive maximal growth in the triceps long head (~2/3 of upper-arm triceps mass).

⚡ If Machine Taken: If overhead cable station is occupied, perform Cable Triceps Pushdown at 20.0 kg (3 × 12–15) or Single-Arm Overhead DB Extension (7.0–8.0 kg DB).

Feel the back-underside of your upper arm (long head near the armpit) stretch tight at the bottom of every rep, then drive your hands forward without flaring elbows outward.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: 20.0 kg pin (Master 12+ reps before +5 kg jump to 25.0 kg)

#5 5. Dumbbell Lateral Raise (+ Set 3 Drop Set)
3 sets x 12–15 reps (Set 3 drops immediately to 2.5 kg x 10–15) | Rest: 60–90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
🎯 5.0 kg per hand (Drop set: 2.5 kg per hand)

Lean torso forward ~10–15°. Raise arms in the scapular plane (15°–30° forward of your sides), stopping strictly at collarbone/shoulder height. Zero torso swing or knee bounce.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: #5 Shortened-Range Lateral Delt Overload & Active Chest Breather. Complements Cable Laterals by peaking tension at the top of the arc while giving chest extra recovery before Pec Deck.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If 5.0 kg DBs are taken, run 3 burnout sets on Cable Lateral Raise (2.5 kg) with a 1-second pause at peak abduction.

Pocket your shoulder blades down before rep 1. Reach your knuckles outward toward the far walls (not upward toward the ceiling) and pull your elbows apart like stretching a tight elastic band.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: 5.0 kg per hand (Drop set: 2.5 kg per hand)

#6 6. Pec Deck Machine Fly
2 sets x 10–12 reps | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
🎯 35.0 kg pin (Fallback: 30.0 kg pin)

Set seat height so handles align directly with mid-chest. Keep elbows softly bent at a fixed angle (do not turn the fly into a press). Control a 3-second eccentric stretch back until handles align with torso, then sweep smoothly inward.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: #6 Pure Pectoral Isolation & Peak Contraction Finisher. Chest is refreshed after Overhead Triceps and DB Laterals so you finish with strong squeeze instead of post-bench rep collapse.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If Pec Deck is busy, execute Standing Cable Chest Fly (set pulleys at shoulder height, 5.0–7.5 kg per side) for 2 × 12–15 reps.

Lead the inward sweep with your inner elbows and wrists together, not your hands. Hold a hard 1-second squeeze when knuckles meet in front of your sternum.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: 35.0 kg pin (Fallback: 30.0 kg pin)
