DAY 6 - PULL B (HIGH-MMC LAT V-TAPER, DENSITY & ARMS)

Evidence-Based Hypertrophy • 5-Day Aesthetic Split
🧠 Coach Protocols & Warm-Up ▼

1. Recommended Session Flow & Sequencing Rationale

  • Follow this 1 $\rightarrow$ 6 flow for maximum back thickness, V-taper lat width, and arm peak:
  • 1. **Primer (Lat Wake-Up)**: **Standing Rope Straight-Arm Pulldown** (2 feeler sets × 12–15 reps). Shuts off the biceps and floods the lats with blood so you feel them contract immediately on your heavy compounds.
  • 2. **Primary Vertical Lat Compound**: **Close-Grip / V-Bar Lat Pulldown** (3 sets × 8–10 reps @ 45–50 kg). Direct sagittal plane line of pull for the mid-to-lower lat fibers.
  • 3. **Heavy Horizontal Density Anchor**: **Seated Cable Row (V-Bar)** (3 sets × 8–10 reps @ 60.0 kg Overload Leap!). Conquered 55kg × 12, 12, 12 on Oct 14—today we test 60kg!
  • 4. **Unilateral Lower Lat Sweep**: **Single-Arm Cable Lat Row** (3 sets × 10–12 reps @ 12.5–15 kg). Deep lat stretch and elbow driving straight into the hip crease.
  • 5. **Posterior Delt Isolation**: **Reverse Pec Deck (Rear Delt Fly)** (3 sets × 10–12 reps @ 15.0 kg). Lock shoulder blades neutral to keep mid-traps out of it.
  • 6. **Bicep Peak Overload**: **EZ-Bar Bicep Curl** (3 sets × 10–12 reps @ 20.0 kg). Strict form, elbows pinned.
  • 7. **Arm Width & Forearms**: **Dumbbell Hammer Curl** (3 sets × 10–12 reps @ 7.5 kg DBs). Brachialis focus for 3D arm width.

2. Equipment Leverage: Lifting Wrist Straps (Mandatory)

  • **Deploy Straps on Movements #2, #3, and #4**: On Oct 14, lifting straps unlocked a 55kg row PR and completely eliminated forearm burnout. Wrap straps tight on all heavy pulldowns and rows today.

3. Dynamic Warm-Up Protocol (5 Mins)

  • **Scapular Hangs / Depressions**: 10 slow reps (hang from bar, depress shoulder blades down without bending elbows).
  • **Band Pull-Aparts**: 20 reps in the transverse plane, squeezing rear delts and rhomboids for 1 second.
  • **Rope Straight-Arm Feeler**: 1 light set of 15 reps @ 12.5 kg to lubricate the shoulder joints.
#1 0. Primer: Standing Rope Straight-Arm Pulldown (Lat Activation)
3 sets x 8–12 reps
🎯 **15.0 kg – 17.5 kg**

Slight torso hinge at 15–20°, soft elbow bend locked in place. Pull rope down in a sweeping arc.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#0 Lat Primer.** Neutralizes the biceps and establishes 10/10 mind-muscle connection before touching heavy weights.

At the bottom, flare your hands apart past your hips and squeeze your armpits into your sides for 1 full second.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **15.0 kg – 17.5 kg**

#2 1. Close-Grip / V-Bar Lat Pulldown
**3 sets x 8–10 reps** | Rest: 120 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
🎯 **45.0 kg (Set 1 feeler/groove) $\rightarrow$ 50.0 kg (Sets 2 & 3 working load)**

Lock thighs tight under pads. Torso leaned back just 10–15°. Drive your elbows straight down into your ribs.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#1 Primary Vertical Lat Driver.** Replaces bodyweight pull-ups to isolate pure lat fibers with locked-in thigh stability.

⚡ If Machine Taken: Neutral-Grip Mag-Grip or Standard Wide Bar with moderate shoulder-width grip (45.0–50.0 kg).

Think about bending your elbows into your back pockets. Do not jerk or heave backward.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **45.0 kg (Set 1 feeler/groove) $\rightarrow$ 50.0 kg (Sets 2 & 3 working load)**

#3 2. Seated Cable Row (V-Bar / Close Grip)
**3 sets x 8–10 reps** | Rest: 120 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
🎯 **60.0 kg pin (OVERLOAD LEAP: 8–10 reps)**. *Fallback*: **55.0 kg pin** (10–12 reps if 60kg form breaks).

Torso upright at ~85° (minimal momentum). Full stretch forward, initiate pull by setting shoulder blades, then drive elbows backward.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#2 Heavy Horizontal Back Density Anchor.** Primary mid-back and lower lat builder.

⚡ If Machine Taken: Chest-Supported T-Bar Row Machine (40.0–45.0 kg) or Chest-Supported Row Machine (45.0–50.0 kg).

Squeeze your mid-back for 1 second at peak contraction. Control the eccentric return for 2 seconds.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **60.0 kg pin (OVERLOAD LEAP: 8–10 reps)**. *Fallback*: **55.0 kg pin** (10–12 reps if 60kg form breaks).

#4 3. Single-Arm Cable Lat Row (or Single-Arm High-to-Low Pulldown)
**3 sets x 10–12 reps per side** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **1**
🎯 **12.5 kg – 15.0 kg pin** (Completed 12.5kg × 12 on Oct 14).

Set cable pulley at knee-to-chest height. Sit or kneel. Hand in neutral grip.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#3 Unilateral Lower Lat Sweep.** Matches your natural ribcage angle and fixes side-to-side imbalances.

⚡ If Machine Taken: 1-Arm Dumbbell Row with hand braced on bench (15.0–17.5 kg DB).

Let the cable pull your arm forward for a deep lat stretch. Pull elbow back directly **into your hip crease**. Keep elbow tucked against your side.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **12.5 kg – 15.0 kg pin** (Completed 12.5kg × 12 on Oct 14).

#5 4. Reverse Pec Deck (Rear Delt Fly)
**3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
🎯 **15.0 kg pin** (Oct 14: 15kg × 12, 12, 9 $\rightarrow$ Target today: 12, 12, 12!).

Chest glued against pad, handles at shoulder height. Slight elbow bend locked.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#4 Posterior Delt Hypertrophy & Posture Restoration.**

⚡ If Machine Taken: Cable Face Pulls with rope (15.0–17.5 kg) or Incline DB Rear Delt Flyes (4.0–5.0 kg DBs).

Sweep arms wide toward room corners. Do not pinch shoulder blades together—isolate the rear delts.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **15.0 kg pin** (Oct 14: 15kg × 12, 12, 9 $\rightarrow$ Target today: 12, 12, 12!).

#6 5. EZ-Bar Bicep Curl
**3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **1**
🎯 **20.0 kg** (Oct 14: 20kg × 15, 12, 11).

Stand tall, elbows pinned to sides. Hinge strictly at the elbow joint without swinging hips.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#5 Direct Bicep Loading.** High mechanical tension for the biceps brachii.

⚡ If Machine Taken: Incline DB Curls (7.5 kg DBs) or Standing Cable Curls (15.0–17.5 kg).

Squeeze biceps at the top for 1 second, lower under 2-second control.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **20.0 kg** (Oct 14: 20kg × 15, 12, 11).

#7 6. Dumbbell Hammer Curl
**3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **0 (Failure on last set)**
🎯 **7.5 kg DBs per hand** (Oct 14: 7.5kg × 10 reps).

Neutral grip (thumbs up). Upper arms locked against ribs.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#6 Brachialis & Forearm Width Finisher.**

⚡ If Machine Taken: Cable Rope Hammer Curls (17.5–20.0 kg pin).

Squeeze forearms and outer biceps at the top. Strict 3-second negative.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **7.5 kg DBs per hand** (Oct 14: 7.5kg × 10 reps).
