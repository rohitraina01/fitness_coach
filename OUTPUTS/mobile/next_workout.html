💡 COACHING MASTERCLASS: WHY V-BAR IS PURE LAT ▼

1. The 30° vs. 45° Incline Paradox

  • **The Gym Trap**: Most gym benches default to 45° or even 60°. At 45°+, EMG studies show that recruitment shifts dramatically away from the clavicular pectoralis and directly into the **Anterior Deltoids**!
  • **The Sweet Spot**: 30° (typically the second notch on adjustable commercial benches). At 30°, clavicular pec fiber activation peaks while front delt recruitment is minimized.

2. How to Eliminate Shoulder Clicking on Incline Pressing

  • **Scapular Depression**: Before un-racking, pull your shoulder blades *down* toward your butt pockets and pinch them into the bench pad.
  • **Elbow Angle**: Never flare elbows out 90° like a letter "T". Tuck elbows at 45°–60° from your ribs (like an arrow "$\rightarrow$"). This keeps the supraspinatus tendon out of the subacromial pinch zone.
  • **Bottom Stretch**: Lower until dumbbells touch the level of your armpits. Do not bounce or drop into passive joint hang. Press up and slightly inward in a smooth arc.
