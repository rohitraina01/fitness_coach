DAY 3 - LEGS, SIX-PACK CORE & POSTURE

First leg session after a 1-year layoff. Strict "Zero-Glute" biomechanics applied. Acclimatization protocol: stop all sets at 2–3 RIR to prevent crippling DOMS while establishing clean baseline numbers.
🧠 Coach Protocols & Warm-Up ▼

1. Dynamic Warm-Up Protocol (5–7 Mins)

  • **Step 1: Light Cardio**: 3–5 minutes on treadmill (incline walk) or stationary bike to circulate synovial fluid in knees.
  • **Step 2: Leg Swings**: 15 reps front-to-back & 15 reps side-to-side per leg (loosens tight hip capsules).
  • **Step 3: Ankle Mobility**: 15 rocks per ankle against wall (primes dorsiflexion so knees track forward smoothly over toes).
  • **Step 4: Specific Sets**: Empty sled $\times$ 12, Sled + 20kg $\times$ 8 directly on Leg Press before loading working weight.

2. 1-Year Layoff Safety Ceiling: 2–3 RIR

  • **Do NOT train to muscular failure (0 RIR) today.** Eccentric muscle trauma on deconditioned legs causes extreme DOMS that can cripple you for 5 days.
  • Leave **2 to 3 clean reps in reserve (2–3 RIR)** on every working set today. Treat this as an Acclimatization & Baseline Calibration session.

3. The "Zero-Glutes" Biomechanical Guardrail

  • 9 hours of daily desk sitting causes shortened hip flexors (psoas) and Anterior Pelvic Tilt (APT), pushing glutes outward and protruding the lower belly.
  • Standard leg exercises (squats, RDLs, hip thrusts, lunges) hyper-develop the gluteus maximus, widening the hips and worsening the feminine pear shape.
  • Today's session uses 0 squats, 0 RDLs, 0 hip thrusts. We load pure quad sweep and knee flexion only.

4. Six-Pack & Core Execution Protocol

  • For six-pack bricks: Kneeling cable crunches require curling the ribcage toward the pelvis, NOT hip hinging.
  • For flat lower belly & APT reversal: Deadbugs require keeping the lower back glued 100% flat to the floor to brace the transverse abdominis.
#1 1. Leg Press (Narrow / Mid-Low Platform Stance)
**3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: **2–3 (Safety Ceiling)**

Narrow, hip-width stance on lower half of platform. Lower until knees hit 90°. Never allow lower back to peel off pad.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

Don't just push the platform up with your hips. Think about *pushing the floor away with your mid-foot while driving your knees outward over your second toe*. Stop strictly when knees hit 90°—if you go deeper, your pelvis tucks under ('butt wink'), turning off your quads and overloading your lower spine. Keep hips glued flat. Your quads will do 100% of the work while your glutes remain completely quiet.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **Sled + 40 to 50 kg total** (20–25 kg plate per side). Warm-up: Sled x 12, Sled + 20kg x 8.

#2 2. Leg Extension Machine
**3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**

Pure quad isolation (0.0% glute recruitment). 1s pause at peak extension, 3s controlled descent.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

Pull back against handles to lock your hips flat into the seat so your butt doesn't lift. Do not fling the weight with momentum. Kick your shins upward towards the ceiling. At peak extension, squeeze and lock your teardrop (vastus medialis) and upper quad like you're flexing hard for a photo for 1 full second. Then resist the weight down for a slow 3-second negative. 0.0% glute recruitment.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **25 to 35 kg** (Pin 4 to 6 on standard stack).

#3 3. Seated or Lying Leg Curl
**3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**

Pure knee flexion (0% hip extension / zero glutes). Slow 3s eccentric return.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

**Dorsiflex your feet** (pull your toes up toward your shins throughout the entire set). If your toes point down, your calves assist the lift; dorsiflexing completely removes the calves and isolates the hamstrings. Drag your heels directly into the back of your thighs and squeeze. Never arch your lower back to yank the pad down.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **20 to 30 kg** (Pin 3 to 5).

#4 4. Standing Calf Raise
**4 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **1**

Full range of motion with 2-second dead pause at the bottom stretch on every single rep.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

The Achilles tendon acts like a giant rubber band designed to bounce weight up effortlessly. To actually force calf muscle hypertrophy, sink into the deepest stretch possible and **count 'one-one-thousand, two-one-thousand' at a dead stop**. Then drive straight up by pushing hard through the ball of your **big toe**, not the outside edge of your foot. Squeeze the calves at the top for 1 second.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **30 to 40 kg** on machine (or holding 15–20 kg DB standing on an elevated block).

#5 5. Kneeling Cable Crunch (Six-Pack Hypertrophy)
**3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**

Lock hips fixed at 90°. Roll spine into a ball, curling ribs down into pelvis. Squeeze abs for 1s.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

**The #1 mistake is sitting back onto your heels or bending at the hips.** Lock your hips and thighs completely motionless at a 90° angle. Hold the rope beside your ears. Imagine your pelvis is an anchor: actively curl your ribcage down and roll your spine into a tight ball toward your belly button. Exhale all your oxygen at the bottom and crush your abs like you are bracing for a punch.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **25 to 35 kg** on cable stack.

#6 6. Deadbugs or Hanging Knee Raise (Anti-APT Lower Core)
**3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **1**

Mandatory Anti-APT movement. Glue lower back 100% flat against floor/pad with zero gap.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

Imagine someone trying to pull a ₹500 note from under your lower spine—press down so hard into the floor that nobody can slide it out. Slowly extend opposite arm and leg while pulling your tailbone slightly up into a posterior pelvic tilt. If your lower back arches even 1 millimeter, stop the rep immediately. If doing Hanging Knee Raises: do not swing; tuck knees up and curl your pelvis up toward your sternum to activate lower abs.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: Bodyweight.

#7 7. Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch (Desk Posture Reset)
**2 sets x 45–60 sec per leg** | Rest: 30 sec | Target RIR: —

Drop rear knee to mat, tuck pelvis into posterior tilt, squeeze rear glute forward.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

Do not just lunge forward and arch your lower back—that bypasses the psoas completely. In the half-kneeling position, tuck your tailbone under like a dog tucking its tail, and **actively squeeze the glute of the trailing back leg as hard as possible**. You will instantly feel a sharp, targeted release in the front crease of your hip pocket. Hold and breathe deeply into your belly for 60 seconds per leg.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: Bodyweight.
