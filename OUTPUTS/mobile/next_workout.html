DAY 5 - PUSH B (LATERAL DELT SPECIALIZATION & TRICEPS)

Evidence-Based Hypertrophy • 5-Day Aesthetic Split
🧠 Coach Protocols & Warm-Up ▼

1. Recommended Session Flow & Sequencing Rationale

  • Follow this 1 $\rightarrow$ 6 flow if stations are free. If machines are occupied, you can swap (e.g. Pec Deck Fly before Incline Press as pre-exhaustion) and note the swap when logging:
  • 1. **Incline Machine Press or Incline DB Press**: Heavy mechanical tension compound. Must be performed when front delts and triceps are completely fresh.
  • 2. **Cable Lateral Raise**: Priority hypertrophy driver for capped shoulders. Constant tension profile across the entire range.
  • 3. **Pec Deck Machine Fly**: Pure sternal chest metabolic stress. Takes chest close to failure with zero triceps involvement.
  • 4. **DB Lateral Raise (Drop Set on final set)**: High-rep pump and burn out to recruit high-threshold motor units.
  • 5. **Overhead Cable Triceps Ext**: Long head isolation. Places triceps under extreme mechanical tension at full shoulder flexion.
  • 6. **Rope Triceps Pushdown**: Lateral and medial head finisher with hard peak contraction.

2. Dynamic Warm-Up Protocol (5–7 Mins)

  • **Step 1: Scapular Wall Slides**: 15 slow reps (wrists and elbows flat against wall; activates lower traps and stabilizes serratus).
  • **Step 2: Band Pull-Aparts / Light DB Warmup**: 20 reps in the scapular plane with thumbs pointed up.
  • **Step 3: Rotator Cuff External Rotations**: 15 reps per side with light cable or 2.5kg dumbbell (primes infraspinatus and teres minor).
  • **Step 4: Exercise-Specific Warm-Up**: Incline DB 12.5kg $\times$ 10, 15kg $\times$ 6 before loading working weight.

3. Mind-Muscle Connection (MMC) Master Cues

  • **The "Pouring Water / Pulling Apart" Cue**: Never think about lifting the dumbbells *up*. Think about **reaching your knuckles toward the far walls and pulling your elbows apart**.
  • **Scapular Plane Angle**: Keep dumbbells angled ~15–30° forward (in the scapular plane), not pure lateral. This prevents the supraspinatus from impinging against the acromion.
  • **Trap Elimination**: Push your shoulder blades *down into your back pockets* before starting each rep. Stop the rep at collarbone height—going higher engages 100% upper traps.
  • **Deep Stretch Cue**: Let the rope/bar drop deep behind your head until your forearms touch your biceps for a full 1-second stretch under load.
  • **Elbow Flare**: Allow elbows to flare naturally ~30° outward; forcing elbows tight to your ears strains the ulnar nerve. Pin upper arms in space and hinge purely from the elbow.
#1 1. Incline DB Press (or Incline Machine Press)
**3 sets x 8–10 reps** | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
🎯 **22.5 kg DBs (Overload Leap: 6–8 reps) OR 20.0 kg DBs (Solidify PR: 10, 10, 10 reps)**

Bench set strictly at 30° (higher shifts load to front delts). Retract scapulae, 45° elbow tuck. Deep chest stretch at bottom.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#1 Heavy Compound First.** Demands peak motor unit recruitment when pressing musculature is 100% fresh.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If 20kg/22.5kg DBs are busy, use Incline Chest Machine (Pin: 45.0–50.0 kg). If DB bench taken, use Flat DB Press (22.5 kg).

Drive biceps across chest at top without banging dumbbells. Keep ribcage high and expanded.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **22.5 kg DBs (Overload Leap: 6–8 reps) OR 20.0 kg DBs (Solidify PR: 10, 10, 10 reps)**

#2 2. Cable Lateral Raise
**4 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
🎯 **2.5 kg – 5.0 kg** (Plate 1 or 2 on low cable). *If cables taken*: **6.0 kg DBs** (or 5.0 kg with 2s top pause).

Set pulley at wrist/hip height. Stand tall, lead with elbow, hold peak contraction for 1 full second.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#2 Priority Delt Hypertrophy.** Constant tension resistance profile across full ROM.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If cable station is fully occupied, perform Single-Arm Leaning DB Lateral Raise (5.0 kg or 6.0 kg DB).

Reach knuckles outward toward the walls; keep shoulder blades pinned down in your back pockets to completely eliminate upper traps.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **2.5 kg – 5.0 kg** (Plate 1 or 2 on low cable). *If cables taken*: **6.0 kg DBs** (or 5.0 kg with 2s top pause).

#3 3. Pec Deck Machine Fly
**3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
🎯 **35.0 kg pin** (Up +5kg from 30kg). *Fallback if heavy*: **30.0 kg pin** for 12–15 reps with 2s squeeze.

Seat adjusted so handles align with mid-sternum. 3-second slow eccentric descent. 1-second chest crush at contraction.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#3 Pure Sternal Chest Isolation.** Delivers deep stretch and pump without fatiguing triceps.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If Pec Deck is taken, use Low-to-High Cable Crossover (10–12.5 kg/side) or Flat DB Flyes (10.0–12.5 kg DBs).

Think about bringing your inner biceps together to touch your ribcage. Do not let shoulders roll forward at the stretch.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **35.0 kg pin** (Up +5kg from 30kg). *Fallback if heavy*: **30.0 kg pin** for 12–15 reps with 2s squeeze.

#4 4. DB Lateral Raise (Drop Set on Final Set)
**3 sets x 12–15 reps (Set 3 is a Drop Set)** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0**
🎯 **5.0 kg DBs** (Sets 1–2: 5kg × 12–15 reps; Set 3: 5kg to failure → immediate drop to **2.5 kg or 3.0 kg DBs** to failure). *If 5kg taken*: **6.0 kg DBs** (10–12 reps) or **4.0 kg DBs** (18–20 reps).

Slight 15° forward torso lean. Sweep outward toward room corners in the scapular plane.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#4 High-Rep Metabolic Delt Finisher.** Recruits high-threshold motor units under extreme burn.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If 5kg DBs taken, use 6kg DBs or perform Plate Lateral Raises with 5kg plates.

Collarbone height only. Zero hip momentum. Drop 40–50% load immediately on set 3 without resting more than 5 seconds.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **5.0 kg DBs** (Sets 1–2: 5kg × 12–15 reps; Set 3: 5kg to failure → immediate drop to **2.5 kg or 3.0 kg DBs** to failure). *If 5kg taken*: **6.0 kg DBs** (10–12 reps) or **4.0 kg DBs** (18–20 reps).

#5 5. Overhead Cable Triceps Extension
**3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
🎯 **17.5 kg – 20.0 kg pin** on cable stack (Up from 15kg). *Fallback*: **15.0 kg pin** for 12–14 strict reps.

Cable pulley set at head height. Step forward into a stable lunge. Keep elbows pinned in place and flare 30° out. Full lockout.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#5 Long-Head Triceps Builder.** Places long head under maximal mechanical stretch at full shoulder flexion.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If cable stack is taken, do Seated Incline DB Overhead Extension with a single **15.0 kg or 17.5 kg DB** held with both hands.

Drop forearms deep behind your neck until biceps touch forearms. Squeeze triceps hard to straighten arms over your head.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **17.5 kg – 20.0 kg pin** on cable stack (Up from 15kg). *Fallback*: **15.0 kg pin** for 12–14 strict reps.

#6 6. Rope Triceps Pushdown
**3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **0**
🎯 **22.5 kg pin** (Up from 20kg). *Fallback*: **20.0 kg pin** for 12–15 reps with 1s lockout hold.

Pin elbows firmly against your ribcage. At full downward extension, flare the rope ends outward toward your outer thighs.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#6 Arm Hypertrophy Finisher.** Pure lockout mechanical pump for lateral and medial heads.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If rope is missing, use Straight-Bar Cable Pushdown (25.0 kg) or V-Bar Pushdown (22.5 kg).

Lock the elbows completely and hold for 1 full second at peak contraction. Control the return to 90° without letting elbows drift forward.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **22.5 kg pin** (Up from 20kg). *Fallback*: **20.0 kg pin** for 12–15 reps with 1s lockout hold.
