DAY 5 - PUSH B (LATERAL DELT SPECIALIZATION & LONG-HEAD TRICEPS)

Evidence-Based Hypertrophy • 5-Day Aesthetic Split
🧠 Coach Protocols & Warm-Up ▼

1. Recommended Session Flow & Sequencing Rationale

  • Follow this 1 $\rightarrow$ 6 flow for maximum lateral deltoid capping, clavicular upper chest shelf, and long-head triceps stretch:
  • 1. **Primary Upper Chest Compound**: **Incline DB Bench Press** (3 working sets × 8–10 reps @ 20.0–22.5 kg). Set 1 top-set probe at 22.5 kg (Oct 19 hit 10 reps); Sets 2–3 anchored at 20.0 kg.
  • 2. **Long-Head Triceps Stretch Driver**: **Overhead Cable Triceps Ext** (3 sets × 10–12 reps @ 15.0–20.0 kg pin). Deep stretch behind neck while shoulder joint is lubricated from pressing.
  • 3. **Continuous-Tension Lateral Delt Builder**: **Cable Lateral Raise** (4 sets × 12–15 reps @ 2.5–5.0 kg). Oct 16 smashed rep ceiling (16 reps @ 2.5kg). Test 5.0 kg on Set 1, back off to 2.5 kg for sets 2–4.
  • 4. **Sternal Chest Peak Contraction**: **Pec Deck Machine Fly** (3 sets × 10–12 reps @ 35.0 kg pin). Smashed 35kg for 12, 12, 10 on Oct 16. Conquer 12, 12, 12 today!
  • 5. **Lateral Delt Burnout Finisher**: **DB Lateral Raise (Drop Set Finisher)** (3 sets × 12–15 reps @ 5.0 kg $\rightarrow$ drop set to 2.5 kg on final set). Strict side delts; no trap shrugging.
  • 6. **Medial/Lateral Triceps Finisher**: **Cable Triceps Pushdown** (2–3 sets × 12–15 reps @ 20.0 kg pin). Lock out elbows and pump maximum blood into arm horseshoes.

2. Biomechanical Guardrails (Anti-Trap Shrugging & Shoulder Protection)

  • **Zero Trap Shrugging on Lateral Raises**: Do NOT shrug up toward your ears. Depress shoulder blades down into your back pockets. Reach your knuckles outward toward the room corners.
  • **Gym Dumbbell Ground Truth Reminder**: There are NO 6.0 kg dumbbells in your gym (only 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8 kg). Do NOT jump from 5kg to 7kg on DB lateral raises (+40% jump causes immediate trap shrugging). Build rep volume on 5.0 kg (up to 15–20 reps) and use Cable Lateral Raises for incremental micro-overload.
  • **Incline Bench Angle Lock**: Exactly 30° (or second pin from flat). Any angle higher than 30° shifts tension from upper pecs directly onto anterior deltoids.
  • **waistband & Elbow Path**: Tuck elbows to a 45° angle on Incline DB Press. Never flare elbows to 90°.

3. Gym Duration Guardrail: 50–55 Minutes Max

  • **Hard Session Cap**: Exactly 6 exercises, 17 working sets + 1 drop set.
  • Rest 150s on Incline DB Press, 90s on Overhead Triceps and Pec Deck Fly, 60–90s on Lateral Raises and Pushdowns. Move with intent.
#1 1. Incline Dumbbell Press
**3 sets x 8–10 reps** | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
🎯 **20.0 kg – 22.5 kg per hand**

Bench set to 30° incline. Retract shoulder blades, plant feet firmly. Dumbbells tucked at 45° angle to torso. Controlled 3-second descent until DB handles touch outer chest.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#1 Primary Upper Chest Mass Driver.** Maximum motor unit recruitment for the upper chest shelf while shoulders and triceps are fresh.

⚡ If Machine Taken: Incline Machine Chest Press (40.0–50.0 kg pin).

Drive elbows inward and upward toward your chin to shorten the clavicular pectoralis. Squeeze upper chest at peak without banging dumbbells together.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **20.0 kg – 22.5 kg per hand**

#2 2. Overhead Cable Triceps Extension
**3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
🎯 **15.0 kg – 20.0 kg pin**

Set pulley to upper chest/neck height. Rope attached. Take staggered stance, lean torso forward 30°. Keep elbows tucked alongside ears.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#2 Long-Head Triceps Stretch Driver.** Places the long head in maximum stretch while shoulders are warm from pressing.

⚡ If Machine Taken: Overhead Dumbbell Extension (15.0–17.5 kg single dumbbell behind head).

Allow rope to pull hands deep behind your neck for a 2-second loaded stretch. Drive forearms forward into full extension, spreading the rope ends wide at lockout. Squeeze triceps for 1 full second.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **15.0 kg – 20.0 kg pin**

#3 3. Cable Lateral Raise
**4 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
🎯 **2.5 kg – 5.0 kg pin**

Set pulley to wrist/knee height. Stand slightly side-on to cable stack, lean 10–15° away from station. Grip cable collar or D-handle.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#3 Constant-Tension Lateral Delt Isolation.** Constant tension across the entire range of motion, particularly at the bottom where dumbbells provide zero tension.

⚡ If Machine Taken: Incline Bench Dumbbell Lateral Raise (3.0–4.0 kg DBs, chest against 30° incline).

Sweep your hand out toward the wall corners, NOT straight up. Stop exactly at collarbone height. Shoulder blade stays pinned down—do NOT shrug traps!

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **2.5 kg – 5.0 kg pin**

#4 4. Pec Deck Machine Fly
**3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
🎯 **35.0 kg pin**

Adjust seat so handles/elbow pads align with mid-chest. Keep chest proud, shoulder blades pinned against the back pad. Soft bend in elbows locked rigid throughout.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#4 Isolated Pectoral Horizontal Adduction.** Heavy mechanical stretch and inner-sternal contraction with zero triceps involvement.

⚡ If Machine Taken: Flat Dumbbell Fly or Cable Crossover (10.0–12.5 kg).

Think "bring your inner biceps together across your chest". 2-second controlled eccentric stretch; violently hug a giant barrel at peak contraction and hold for 1 full second.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **35.0 kg pin**

#5 5. Dumbbell Lateral Raise (Drop Set Finisher)
**3 sets x 12–15 reps + 1 drop set to failure** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **0**
🎯 **5.0 kg DBs** $\rightarrow$ immediate drop to **2.5 kg DBs** on Set 3.

Hinge forward 10° at the hips. Lead with elbows, keep pinkies level with thumbs ("pour the water" cue). Stop at shoulder height.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#5 Deltoid Capping & Metabolic Exhaustion.** Final high-volume pump to maximize sarcoplasmic volume in the lateral delts.

Imagine pushing the walls away from your body. Burn through the lactic threshold; zero swinging from knees or hips.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **5.0 kg DBs** $\rightarrow$ immediate drop to **2.5 kg DBs** on Set 3.

#6 6. Cable Triceps Pushdown
**2–3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
🎯 **20.0 kg pin**

Straight bar or V-bar attached to high pulley. Pin elbows tight against side ribs. Lean forward slightly at torso.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#6 Medial & Lateral Triceps Horseshoe Finisher.** Direct elbow extension lockout to finish off arm volume.

⚡ If Machine Taken: Overhead Triceps Pushdown or Rope Pushdown.

Press straight down, locking out elbows with zero movement from upper arms. Squeeze the triceps horseshoe at bottom lockout for 1 full second.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **20.0 kg pin**
