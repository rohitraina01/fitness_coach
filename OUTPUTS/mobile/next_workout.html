DAY 3 - LEGS & POSTURE (QUAD SWEEP, CALVES & ANTI-APT)

Evidence-Based Hypertrophy • 5-Day Aesthetic Split
#1 1. High-Foot Leg Press (Quad Sweep Focus)
  • **Warm-Up**: 2 sets (e.g. 50kg x 12, 80kg x 6).
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: 1–2
  • **Targets**:
  • **Floor**: 3 sets x 10 reps with full controlled knee flexion.
  • **Main**: 3 sets x 11–12 reps with smooth continuous tension.
  • **Stretch**: 3 sets x 12 reps.
  • **Form Cue**: High-foot placement shifts load away from the patellar tendon onto the quad sweep. Keep lumbar glued to seat pad—zero butt tuck or spinal rounding.
#2 2. Seated Leg Extension Machine
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
  • **Targets**:
  • **Floor**: 3 sets x 12 reps with strict tempo.
  • **Main**: 3 sets x 14–15 reps with distinct 2-second squeeze at top lockout.
  • **Stretch**: 3 sets x 15 reps + 5 lengthened partials on Set 3.
  • **Form Cue**: Squeeze thighs against pads. Do not kick or swing using hip flexor momentum.
#3 3. Standing Dumbbell / Machine Calf Raise
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
  • **Targets**:
  • **Floor**: 3 sets x 12 reps.
  • **Main**: 3 sets x 13–15 reps with 2-second dead stop at the bottom stretch.
  • **Stretch**: 3 sets x 15 reps.
  • **Form Cue**: Dead stop at the bottom completely eliminates Achilles tendon elastic bounce, forcing gastrocnemius fibers to perform 100% of the work.
  • **Zero Barbell Squats or Deadlifts**: Prohibited per your Anti-APT program to prevent excessive glute hypertrophy.
