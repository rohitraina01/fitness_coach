DAY 6 - PULL B (BACK DENSITY, REAR DELTS & ARM THICKNESS)

Evidence-Based Hypertrophy • 5-Day Aesthetic Split
🧠 Coach Protocols & Warm-Up ▼

1. Recommended Session Flow & Sequencing Rationale

  • Follow this 1 $\rightarrow$ 6 flow for maximum back thickness, V-taper lat width, and arm peak:
  • 1. **Pull-Up (Bodyweight or Assisted)**: Vertical pulling compound #1. Must be performed when central nervous system and grip are 100% fresh.
  • 2. **Seated Cable Row (V-Bar or Neutral Close Grip)**: Heavy horizontal density anchor. Double-progression unlocked on Oct 14 (55kg × 12, 12, 12); today we test the **60.0 kg Overload Leap**!
  • 3. **One-Arm Dumbbell Row**: Unilateral lat sweep. Eliminates side-to-side asymmetries and stretches lats deeply through full ROM.
  • 4. **Reverse Pec Deck Machine (Rear Delt Fly) or Cable Face Pull**: Pure posterior delt isolation. Critical for 3D shoulder capping and pulling hunched desk shoulders back.
  • 5. **Incline Dumbbell Curl**: Long-head bicep builder. Places biceps under extreme mechanical stretch behind the torso at a 45° incline.
  • 6. **Dumbbell Hammer Curl (or Reverse Cable Curl)**: Brachialis and forearm thickness finisher to create massive upper arm width.

2. Equipment Leverage: Lifting Wrist Straps (Mandatory)

  • **Deploy Straps on Movements #1, #2, and #3**: On Oct 14, lifting straps unlocked a 55kg row PR and eliminated forearm pump limits. Use straps on all heavy pulling sets today so your back fails before your fingers.

3. Dynamic Warm-Up Protocol (5–7 Mins)

  • **Step 1: Scapular Dead Hangs & Retractions**: 10 slow reps (hang from bar, depress and pull shoulder blades down without bending elbows).
  • **Step 2: Band Pull-Aparts**: 20 reps in the transverse plane, squeezing rear delts and rhomboids for 1 second.
  • **Step 3: Rotator Cuff / Face Pull Feelers**: 15 reps with very light cable (10kg) to lubricate shoulder capsules.
  • **Step 4: Seated Row Warm-Up Ramp**: 30.0 kg × 10 reps, 45.0 kg × 6 reps before loading 55kg/60kg working weight.

4. Mind-Muscle Connection (MMC) Master Cues

  • **The "Drive Elbows to Hip Crease" Cue**: Do not think about pulling the handle to your chest with your hands. Think about **driving your elbows backward into your rear pockets**.
  • **Scapular Rhythm**: Allow shoulder blades to drift forward into a full stretch at the bottom; initiate the pull by pinching shoulder blades together *before* elbows bend.
  • **Knuckles to Corners**: Reach your knuckles outward toward the room corners.
  • **Do Not Pinch Scapulae**: Keep shoulder blades neutral/flared. If you pinch your shoulder blades together, your mid-traps and rhomboids hijack 80% of the rear delt tension.
  • **Elbows Behind Torso**: Set bench to 45–60°. Keep upper arms perpendicular to the floor, slightly behind torso.
  • **Zero Shoulder Swiveling**: Hinge strictly at the elbow joint. Do not allow elbows to drift forward during the curl.
#1 1. Pull-Up (Bodyweight or Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown)
**3 sets x 4–6 reps** (or 8–10 reps if using Lat Pulldown) | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
🎯 **0.0 kg (Bodyweight: 4–6 strict reps) OR 50.0 kg Lat Pulldown (8–10 reps)**

Dead hang at the bottom. Depress scapulae down first, then drive elbows down to ribs. Pull upper chest up toward the bar.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#1 Vertical Pulling Compound First.** Recruits maximum motor units when completely fresh.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If pull-up bar is occupied or shoulders feel stiff, perform Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown (Pin: 45.0–50.0 kg) or Assisted Pull-Up Machine (-20kg pin).

Imagine bending the bar across your chest. Eliminate swinging or leg kicking.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **0.0 kg (Bodyweight: 4–6 strict reps) OR 50.0 kg Lat Pulldown (8–10 reps)**

#2 2. Seated Cable Row (V-Bar or Neutral Close Grip)
**3 sets x 8–10 reps** | Rest: 120 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
🎯 **60.0 kg pin (OVERLOAD LEAP: 8–10 reps) OR 55.0 kg pin (Solidify: 10–12 reps with 1s pause)**

Torso stationary at ~85° upright (no excessive swinging). Wrap wrist straps tight around V-bar. Full stretch forward, drive elbows past ribs.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#2 Heavy Horizontal Back Density Anchor.** Primary mid-back and lower lat builder.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If low cable row is busy, use Chest-Supported T-Bar Row Machine (40.0–45.0 kg) or Chest-Supported Row Machine (45.0–50.0 kg).

Squeeze shoulder blades together for 1 full second at full contraction. Let lats flare out wide during the 2-second negative return.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **60.0 kg pin (OVERLOAD LEAP: 8–10 reps) OR 55.0 kg pin (Solidify: 10–12 reps with 1s pause)**

#3 3. One-Arm Dumbbell Row
**3 sets x 8–10 reps per side** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
🎯 **17.5 kg – 20.0 kg DB (Overload Leap: 8–10 reps)**. *Fallback*: **15.0 kg DB** (12 reps with 2s negative).

One knee and hand on flat bench (or standing staggered stance with one hand braced on dumbbell rack). Flat neutral spine.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#3 Unilateral Lat Sweep & Anti-Asymmetry.** Gives maximum lat stretch without spinal compression.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If dumbbells are occupied, use Single-Arm Low Cable Row (15.0–17.5 kg) or Chest-Supported DB Row (15.0 kg DBs).

Pull dumbbell in an arc toward your hip crease (not straight up into armpit). Keep elbow tucked tight against your side.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **17.5 kg – 20.0 kg DB (Overload Leap: 8–10 reps)**. *Fallback*: **15.0 kg DB** (12 reps with 2s negative).

#4 4. Reverse Pec Deck (Rear Delt Fly)
**3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
🎯 **15.0 kg pin (Solidify: 12, 12, 12 reps)**. *Fallback*: **12.5 kg pin** (15 reps with 2s contraction).

Chest glued against pad, seat adjusted so handles sit at shoulder height. Slight elbow bend locked in place.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#4 Posterior Delt Hypertrophy & Posture Restoration.** Balances heavy pressing and pulls shoulders back.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If machine is occupied, perform Cable Face Pulls with rope (15.0–17.5 kg) or Incline Prone DB Rear Delt Flyes (4.0–5.0 kg DBs).

Sweep arms outward in a wide circle. Reach knuckles toward room corners. Keep shoulder blades neutral—do not let mid-traps pinch.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **15.0 kg pin (Solidify: 12, 12, 12 reps)**. *Fallback*: **12.5 kg pin** (15 reps with 2s contraction).

#5 5. Incline Dumbbell Curl
**3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
🎯 **7.5 kg DBs per hand (Primary: 10–12 reps)**. *Fallback*: **5.0 kg DBs** (12–15 reps with 3s eccentric).

Bench inclined at 45–60°. Sit fully back, arms hanging straight down behind torso.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#5 Long-Head Bicep Peak Isolation.** Extreme stretch profile for taller bicep peaks.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If adjustable benches are taken, use Standing Cable Bicep Curls (15.0–17.5 kg) or Preacher Curl Bench with EZ Bar (15.0–17.5 kg).

Supinate wrists (turn pinkies up) as you curl. Keep upper arms completely stationary; curl solely with the biceps without rocking shoulders forward.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **7.5 kg DBs per hand (Primary: 10–12 reps)**. *Fallback*: **5.0 kg DBs** (12–15 reps with 3s eccentric).

#6 6. Dumbbell Hammer Curl (or Cable Rope Hammer Curl)
**3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **0**
🎯 **7.5 kg DBs per hand (Primary: 10–12 reps)**. *Fallback*: **10.0 kg DBs** (6–8 reps) or **5.0 kg DBs** (15 slow reps).

Stand tall with neutral pelvis. Neutral grip (thumbs facing ceiling). Lock elbows against ribs.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: **#6 Arm Thickness & Forearm Hypertrophy.** Recruits brachialis and brachioradialis for dense 3D arm width.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If dumbbells are busy, perform Cable Rope Hammer Curls (17.5–20.0 kg pin).

Squeeze forearms and outer arm hard at peak. Lower under strict 3-second control.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: **7.5 kg DBs per hand (Primary: 10–12 reps)**. *Fallback*: **10.0 kg DBs** (6–8 reps) or **5.0 kg DBs** (15 slow reps).
