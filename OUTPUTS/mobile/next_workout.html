DAY 1 - PUSH A (CYCLE 3 • INTERLEAVED CHEST & DUAL LATERAL DELT WIDTH)

Rule 9 satisfied via Nov 27 Legs & Core. Push A upgraded to interleaved sequencing (Incline DB → Cable Lateral → Smith Bench → DB Lateral → Pec Deck → Triceps Pushdown) so chest presses never stack back-to-back and lateral delts hit both lengthened & shortened profiles.
🧠 Coach Protocols & Warm-Up ▼

1. Why Interleaved Sequencing (No Back-to-Back Chest Presses)

  • Active Chest & Triceps Clearance: Running Incline DB Press and Smith Bench back-to-back leaves local metabolites (H+ ions, triceps fatigue) in your pressing chain, blunting sternal chest output and causing later Pec Deck sets to crash (as seen on Oct 19: 11 → 6 reps). Placing Lateral Raises between every chest movement gives your pecs and triceps 6–8 minutes of pure recovery while hammering your #1 priority muscle.
  • Dual-Profile Lateral Delt Stimulus: Cable Lateral Raise loads the lengthened bottom range (stretch), while DB Lateral Raise loads the shortened top range (peak contraction + drop set). Total = 6 working sets + 1 drop set.
  • Dynamic Warm-Up (3 Minutes Max): 15 arm circles forward/backward, 15 scapular wall slides, then 12.5 kg × 10 reps + 17.5 kg × 4 feeler reps on Incline DB Press before 22.5 kg.

2. Equipment & Progression Guardrails

  • NO 6.0 kg DB in Gym: Rack jumps 5.0 kg → 7.0 kg (+40%). Stay strictly on 5.0 kg DBs for DB Lateral Raises and push rep density (12–15+ reps with 2s eccentrics) + a Set 3 drop set to 2.5 kg.
  • +5.0 kg Cable Stack Increments: On Triceps Pushdown, master 12–15 clean reps across all 3 sets at 20.0 kg before jumping +5 kg to the 25.0 kg pin.
  • RIR Standards: Stop compounds (Incline DB, Smith Bench) at 1–2 RIR. Push isolations (Cable/DB Lateral Raise, Pec Deck, Pushdowns) to 0–1 RIR.

3. Gym Duration Guardrail: 50–55 Minutes Max

  • 17 Working Sets Total: Rest 150 sec on Incline DB & Smith Bench, 60–90 sec on Lateral Raises, Pec Deck, and Pushdowns. Use the app Rest Timer so sets stay tight.
#1 1. Incline Dumbbell Bench Press (30° Bench)
3 sets x 6–10 (Set 1) / 8–12 (Sets 2–3) reps | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: 1–2
🎯 22.5 kg top-set / 20.0 kg back-off (per hand)

Set bench strictly to 30° (second notch)—never 45°. Tuck elbows 45°–60° from torso, lower DBs to outer upper-chest stretch for 2 seconds, drive up smoothly without clacking weights at top.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: #1 Clavicular Upper-Chest Anchor. Executed first while CNS and front delts are 100% fresh.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If adjustable benches are full, start with Smith Machine Bench Press (#3) or use Incline Machine Chest Press (35.0–45.0 kg).

Pin shoulder blades down and back into the bench before unracking. Think about sweeping your biceps inward across your collarbone shelf rather than pushing your hands away.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: 22.5 kg top-set / 20.0 kg back-off (per hand)

#2 2. Cable Lateral Raise
3 sets x 12–15 reps | Rest: 60–90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
🎯 2.5 kg pin (Master 15 reps across all 3 sets before testing 5.0 kg)

Set pulley to wrist/hip height, cable crossing in front of thighs (or behind back). Stand tall, hold upright support lightly with non-working hand, sweep arm out in the scapular plane (15° forward) to shoulder height, and control a 2-second negative without letting the weight stack clank.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: #2 Lengthened-Range Lateral Delt Priority & Active Chest Recovery. Gives pecs/triceps 6–8 minutes of complete metabolic clearance before flat pressing while loading the medial delt under constant cable tension.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If cable stack is occupied, swap order with Smith Machine Bench Press (#3) or perform single-arm Leaning DB Lateral Raise (5.0 kg DB).

Depress your shoulder blade down into your back pocket before every rep. Think about dragging your wrist/knuckles toward the opposite wall, keeping your pinky level with your thumb.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: 2.5 kg pin (Master 15 reps across all 3 sets before testing 5.0 kg)

#3 3. Smith Machine Bench Press (Flat)
3 sets x 6–8 reps | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: 1–2
🎯 42.5 kg – 47.5 kg total (bar + plates)

Align bench so the bar touches your lower sternum / nipple line at the bottom. Plant feet flat into the floor, keep chest proud, and lower under a 2-second controlled descent without bouncing off the chest.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: #3 Refreshed Sternal Chest Mechanical Tension. Executed after Cable Lateral Raises so pecs and triceps are cleared of incline-press fatigue.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If Smith machine is taken, execute Flat Barbell Bench Press (40.0–42.5 kg) or Seated Chest Press Machine (40.0–45.0 kg) for 3 × 8–10 reps.

Imagine trying to bend the bar inward and squeeze your elbows together across your lower chest as you press. Keep shoulders depressed away from your ears.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: 42.5 kg – 47.5 kg total (bar + plates)

#4 4. Dumbbell Lateral Raise (+ Set 3 Drop Set)
3 sets x 12–15 reps (Set 3 drops immediately to 2.5 kg x 10–15) | Rest: 60–90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
🎯 5.0 kg per hand (Drop set: 2.5 kg per hand)

Lean torso forward ~10–15°. Raise arms in the scapular plane (15°–30° forward of your sides), stopping strictly at collarbone/shoulder height. Zero torso swing or knee bounce.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: #4 Shortened-Range Lateral Delt Overload & Active Chest Breather. Complements Cable Laterals by peaking tension at the top of the arc while resting chest before Pec Deck.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If 5.0 kg DBs are taken, run a 4th–6th set on Cable Lateral Raise (2.5 kg) with a 1-second hold at peak abduction.

Pocket your shoulder blades down before rep 1. Reach your knuckles outward toward the far walls (not upward toward the ceiling) and pull your elbows apart like stretching a tight elastic band.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: 5.0 kg per hand (Drop set: 2.5 kg per hand)

#5 5. Pec Deck Machine Fly
2 sets x 10–12 reps | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
🎯 35.0 kg pin (Fallback: 30.0 kg pin)

Set seat height so handles align directly with mid-chest. Keep elbows softly bent at a fixed angle (do not turn the fly into a press). Control a 3-second eccentric stretch back until handles align with torso, then sweep smoothly inward.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: #5 Pure Pectoral Isolation & Lengthened Stretch. Chest is refreshed after DB Lateral Raises so you finish with strong squeeze instead of post-bench rep collapse.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If Pec Deck is busy, execute Standing Cable Chest Fly (set pulleys at shoulder height, 5.0–7.5 kg per side) for 2 × 12–15 reps.

Lead the inward sweep with your inner elbows and wrists together, not your hands. Hold a hard 1-second squeeze when knuckles meet in front of your sternum.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: 35.0 kg pin (Fallback: 30.0 kg pin)

#6 6. Cable Triceps Pushdown (Straight Bar)
3 sets x 12–15 reps | Rest: 60–90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
🎯 20.0 kg pin (No 22.5 kg pin; earn 3 × 12–15 before jumping to 25.0 kg)

Stand tall with slight forward hip hinge, pin upper arms/elbows strictly to your ribs. Press bar smoothly down to full elbow lockout, hold 1 second, and control the return until forearms just clear parallel (90°–100°). Do NOT let elbows drift forward or flare out.

💡 Mind-Muscle Cue & Execution

🔄 Order Rationale: #6 Lateral & Medial Triceps Lockout Finisher. Carves the horseshoe triceps contour while elbows are thoroughly warmed up.

⚡ If Machine Taken: If straight bar is occupied, use Rope Triceps Pushdown at 15.0–20.0 kg (spreading rope apart at bottom lockout) or Overhead Cable Triceps Extension at 20.0 kg.

Think about pushing your pinky-side palms straight into the floor and flexing the outside horseshoe of your arm hard at every lockout.

⚖️ Starting Load Target: 20.0 kg pin (No 22.5 kg pin; earn 3 × 12–15 before jumping to 25.0 kg)
