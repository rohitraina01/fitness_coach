=== DAY 2 - PULL A (LAT WIDTH, UPPER BACK & BICEPS) ===
Active Plan for Phone Clipboard
------------------------------------

[1. Lat Pulldown (Neutral or Moderate Grip)]
  • **Warm-Up**: 2 sets (e.g. 30kg x 12, 40kg x 6).
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 8–10 reps** | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: 1–2
  • **Targets**:
  • **Floor**: 3 sets x 8 reps @ 50.0 kg.
  • **Main**: 3 sets x 9–10 reps @ 50.0 kg (Baseline: 50kg x 9 reps).
  • **Stretch**: 3 sets x 10 reps @ 50.0 kg (Triggers +2.5kg increase to 52.5kg).
  • **Form Cue**: Slight 10–15° torso lean. Drive elbows down directly toward your hip pockets. Full stretch at the top without allowing shoulders to shrug into ears.

[2. Chest-Supported Row Machine]
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 8–10 reps** | Rest: 120 sec | Target RIR: 1–2
  • **Targets**:
  • **Floor**: 3 sets x 8 reps with controlled eccentric.
  • **Main**: 3 sets x 9–10 reps with distinct 1-second squeeze at full contraction.
  • **Stretch**: 3 sets x 10 reps.
  • **Form Cue**: Chest glued flat to pad. Flare elbows out at ~45°. Drive elbows backward and pinch shoulder blades together hard.

[3. Straight-Arm Cable Pulldown]
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
  • **Targets**:
  • **Floor**: 3 sets x 12 reps.
  • **Main**: 3 sets x 13–15 reps with constant tension.
  • **Stretch**: 3 sets x 15 reps.
  • **Form Cue**: Soft bend in elbows, locked in place. Hinge hips slightly. Sweep the bar down in an arc straight into upper thighs using pure lat contraction.

[4. Reverse Pec Deck (Rear Delt Fly)]
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
  • **Targets**:
  • **Floor**: 3 sets x 12 reps.
  • **Main**: 3 sets x 14–15 reps.
  • **Stretch**: 3 sets x 15 reps.
  • **Form Cue**: Handles aligned with shoulder height. Do not squeeze traps—keep upper back neutral and drive outward in a wide arc using rear deltoids.

[5. Standing Cable Bicep Curl]
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
  • **Targets**:
  • **Floor**: 3 sets x 10 reps.
  • **Main**: 3 sets x 11–12 reps.
  • **Stretch**: 3 sets x 12 reps.
  • **Form Cue**: Upper arms pinned vertical to your sides. Zero swinging or hip thrusting. Curl upward smoothly and resist the 3-second eccentric drop.

[6. Dumbbell Hammer Curl]
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 8–10 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: 0–1
  • **Targets**:
  • **Floor**: 3 sets x 8 reps @ 7.5 kg per hand.
  • **Main**: 3 sets x 9–10 reps @ 7.5 kg per hand.
  • **Stretch**: 3 sets x 10 reps @ 7.5 kg per hand.
  • **Form Cue**: Strict neutral grip (thumbs pointing up). Emphasizes the brachialis and brachioradialis for front-arm width and forearm density.
