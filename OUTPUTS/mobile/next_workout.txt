=== DAY 2 - PULL A (LAT WIDTH & BICEP HYPERTROPHY) ===
Note: Evidence-Based Hypertrophy • 5-Day Aesthetic Split
------------------------------------
[COACH PROTOCOLS]
• 1. Recommended Session Flow & Sequencing Rationale:
    - Follow this 1 $\rightarrow$ 6 flow for maximum lat width, upper back thickness, and 3D bicep peaks:
    - 1. **Vertical Lat Width Anchor**: **Lat Pulldown (Neutral or Moderate Grip)** (3 sets × 8–10 reps @ 45.0–50.0 kg). Drive elbows straight down into ribcage pockets. Deploy wrist straps to lock grip.
    - 2. **Horizontal Upper Back Scapular Retraction**: **Chest-Supported Row Machine** (3 sets × 8–10 reps @ 40.0–45.0 kg). Chest glued to pad; eliminate torso sway. Squeeze shoulder blades together for 1s.
    - 3. **Pure Lat Isolation / MMC Stretch**: **Straight-Arm Cable Pulldown** (3 sets × 12–15 reps @ 15.0 kg). High motor-unit recruitment with soft elbows. Sweep bar/rope down into upper thighs.
    - 4. **Rear Delt Fly & Scapular Decoupling**: **Reverse Pec Deck (Rear Delt Fly)** (3 sets × 12–15 reps @ 15.0–20.0 kg). Neutral handles, sweep knuckles outward to room corners.
    - 5. **Bicep Peak & Mechanical Tension**: **EZ-Bar Bicep Curl** (3 sets × 10–12 reps @ 20.0 kg). Strict form with elbows pinned to ribs. No hip swinging.
    - 6. **Brachialis & Forearm Thickness**: **Dumbbell Hammer Curl** (3 sets × 10–12 reps @ 7.0–8.0 kg DBs). Neutral grip. Test 8.0 kg DBs on Set 1 or dominate 7.0 kg with controlled eccentrics.
• 2. Gym Equipment Reality Rules (Applied to Today's Session):
    - **Machine Stacks (+5 kg Steps)**: For cable pulldowns and rows, plates transition to +5 kg increments. On isolations (Straight-Arm Pulldowns, Rear Delts), do NOT jump prematurely if +5 kg degrades form; accumulate reps to 15 first.
    - **Dumbbell Rack (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8 kg)**: There is NO 6.0 kg DB. For Hammer Curls, transition from 7.0 kg directly to 8.0 kg DBs per hand.
    - **Lifting Wrist Straps**: **MANDATORY** on Lat Pulldowns and Chest-Supported Rows. Completely eliminates forearm/grip fatigue so lats take 100% of the load.
• 3. Gym Duration Guardrail: 55–60 Minutes Max:
    - **Zero Marathon Sessions**: Oct 19 was 75m due to talking. Today, keep your phone in your pocket, use the built-in rest timer (120–150s compounds, 90s isolations), and execute in under 60 minutes.
• 4. Dynamic Warm-Up Protocol (5 Mins):
    - **Dead Hang from Pull-Up Bar**: 2 × 30 seconds (Decompresses lumbar spine from 9h desk sitting and activates scapular depressors).
    - **Band Pull-Aparts**: 20 slow reps with 1s squeeze to prime lower traps and rhomboids.
    - **Lat Pulldown Feeler**: 1 light set of 10 reps @ 25.0 kg to groove vertical lat pull line.

[1. Lat Pulldown (Neutral or Moderate Overhand Grip)]
  • **Order Rationale**: **#1 Priority Lat Width Compound.** Hits the lats with maximum mechanical tension while fresh.
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 8–10 reps** | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
  • **Target Weight**: **45.0 kg – 50.0 kg pin** (Oct 18: 45kg × 10, 8 $\rightarrow$ Main Target: Test 50.0 kg for 8–10 reps on Set 1 with straps; back off to 45.0 kg × 8–10).
  • **Gym Floor Swap Option**: Close-Grip V-Bar Lat Pulldown (45.0–50.0 kg) or Assisted Pull-Up Machine (-25 kg).
  • **Warm-Up Ramp**: 25.0 kg × 8 reps, 35.0 kg × 5 reps before working sets.
  • **Form Cue**: Sit upright with a slight 10–15° torso lean. Fasten wrist straps securely around bar. Depress shoulder blades *before* pulling.
  • **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Do NOT pull with your hands. Treat your hands as passive hooks; imagine driving your elbows straight down into your back pockets. Full 2-second stretch at the top to lengthen the lat insertion.

[2. Chest-Supported Row Machine]
  • **Order Rationale**: **#2 Upper Back Scapular Retraction & Lat Thickness.** Eliminates lower back axial fatigue.
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 8–10 reps** | Rest: 120 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
  • **Target Weight**: **40.0 kg – 45.0 kg pin** (Oct 18 Seated Row PR was 65kg; on Chest-Supported Machine target 40.0–45.0 kg).
  • **Gym Floor Swap Option**: Seated Cable Row with V-Bar (60.0–65.0 kg) or One-Arm DB Row on bench (20.0 kg DB).
  • **Form Cue**: Adjust chest pad so your sternum rests flat against it. Keep chest glued to pad—zero bouncing.
  • **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Pull elbows back at a 45° angle. Squeeze your shoulder blades together like cracking a walnut between them at the peak for a full 1-second hold.

[3. Straight-Arm Cable Pulldown]
  • **Order Rationale**: **#3 Pure Lat Isolation & Metabolic Burn.** Isolates lats without bicep assistance.
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
  • **Target Weight**: **15.0 kg pin** (Oct 18: 15.0 kg × 12, 12, 12 $\rightarrow$ Main Target: Push reps to 15, 15, 15 before jumping to 20.0 kg).
  • **Gym Floor Swap Option**: Rope Straight-Arm Pulldown (15.0 kg pin) or DB Pullover on flat bench (15.0–17.5 kg DB).
  • **Form Cue**: Slight hip hinge (20° forward lean). Elbows soft but locked rigidly in place.
  • **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Sweep the bar/rope down in a wide arc toward your thighs. Think about wrapping your lats around your ribcage at the bottom.

[4. Reverse Pec Deck (Rear Delt Fly)]
  • **Order Rationale**: **#4 Posterior Frame 3D Thickness.** Balances heavy pressing and builds 3D delt depth.
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
  • **Target Weight**: **15.0 kg – 20.0 kg pin** (Oct 18: 15.0 kg × 12, 12, 12 $\rightarrow$ Main Target: Test 20.0 kg pin for 8–10 reps, or solidify 15.0 kg × 15 reps).
  • **Gym Floor Swap Option**: Cable Face Pull (15.0–20.0 kg pin) or Incline Prone DB Rear Delt Fly (5.0 kg DBs).
  • **Form Cue**: Adjust seat so handles align with mid-chest/shoulders. Neutral grip (palms facing each other). Keep shoulder blades flat and stationary.
  • **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Push your knuckles *outward toward the side walls*, NOT backward. Do not squeeze shoulder blades together—isolate the rear deltoid cap.

[5. EZ-Bar Bicep Curl]
  • **Order Rationale**: **#5 Primary Bicep Mass Builder.** Direct elbow flexion with wrist-friendly cambered bar.
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **1**
  • **Target Weight**: **20.0 kg bar** (Oct 18: 20.0 kg × 12, 10, 10 $\rightarrow$ Main Target: 20.0 kg × 12, 12, 12 Solidification).
  • **Gym Floor Swap Option**: Standing Cable Bicep Curl (15.0–20.0 kg pin) or Incline Dumbbell Curl (7.0–8.0 kg DBs).
  • **Form Cue**: Pin upper arms against ribcage. Stand tall with core engaged. No backwards torso lean.
  • **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Curl up smoothly, squeeze biceps hard at top for 1 second. Lower under control for 3 full seconds.

[6. Dumbbell Hammer Curl]
  • **Order Rationale**: **#6 Brachialis & Arm Width Anchor.** Builds the muscle underneath the bicep that pushes it up and widens the arm from the front.
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **0**
  • **Target Weight**: **7.0 kg – 8.0 kg DBs per hand** (Oct 18: 15 reps logged $\rightarrow$ Main Target: Test 8.0 kg DBs for 8–10 reps on Set 1; back off to 7.0 kg × 12 reps).
  • **Gym Floor Swap Option**: Rope Cable Hammer Curl (15.0–20.0 kg pin).
  • **Form Cue**: Neutral grip (palms facing each other like holding a hammer). Keep wrists rigid and aligned with forearms.
  • **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Drive thumbs toward your shoulders. Squeeze the outer upper forearm and brachialis at the top of every rep.
