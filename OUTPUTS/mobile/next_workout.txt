=== DAY 6 - LEGS & ANTI-APT CORE (QUAD SWEEP, HAMSTRINGS & PELVIC RESTORATION) ===
Note: Evidence-Based Hypertrophy • 5-Day Aesthetic Split
------------------------------------
[COACH PROTOCOLS]
• 1. Recommended Session Flow & Sequencing Rationale:
    - Follow this 1 $\rightarrow$ 5 flow for maximum quad sweep hypertrophy, hamstring pelvic restoration, diamond calves, and 9h desk APT reversal (0% glute loading):
    - 1. **Primary Quad Mass Driver**: **Leg Press (Narrow / Mid-Low Stance)** (3 sets × 10–12 reps @ 54.0–60.0 kg). Set 1 top-set probe at 60.0 kg (Oct 21 conquered 54kg for 12, 12, 12); Sets 2–3 anchored at 54.0 kg.
    - 2. **Pure Quad Isolation**: **Leg Extension Machine** (3 sets × 12–15 reps @ 10.0–12.0 kg). 1s peak contraction squeeze, 3s eccentric negative.
    - 3. **Pelvic Neutralizer & Knee Flexor**: **Lying Leg Curl Machine** (4 sets × 10–12 reps @ 5.0–7.5 kg). **Dorsiflex feet (toes pulled to shins)** to eliminate gastrocnemius takeover and force pure biceps femoris recruitment.
    - 4. **Achilles Stretch Anchor**: **Standing Calf Raise** (4 sets × 12–15 reps @ 80.0–85.0 kg or loaded machine). **2-second dead stop at maximum stretch** at the bottom to kill elastic recoil.
    - 5. **Anti-APT Core Finisher**: **Floor Crunch & Deadbug Superset** (3 sets × 12–15 reps crunch + 6–8 reps deadbug). Lower back 100% glued flat to floor; curl ribcage to pelvis.
• 2. Biomechanical Guardrails (Anti-APT & 0% Glute Work):
    - **Zero Glute Bias on Leg Press**: Place feet hip-width apart and low-to-mid on the platform. Keep knees tracking directly over toes. Do NOT place feet high and wide (that recruits glutes and adductors). Stop descent before lower back rounds off the back pad ("butt wink").
    - **Dorsiflex Feet on Leg Curls**: Keep ankles locked with toes pulled toward your knees at all times. Plantarflexion (pointing toes) forces your calf muscle (gastrocnemius) to assist, robbing your hamstrings of the tension needed to pull your anteriorly tilted pelvis back to neutral.
    - **Lower Back Glued Flat on Core**: On Floor Crunches and Deadbugs, posterior pelvic tilt is mandatory. Press your lumbar spine into the floor so a hand could not slide under.
• 3. Gym Duration Guardrail: 50–55 Minutes Max:
    - **Hard Session Cap**: Exactly 5 exercises (including core superset). 14 working sets + core.
    - Rest 150s on Leg Press, 90s on Leg Extensions and Leg Curls, 60s on Calves and Core. Move with intent.
------------------------------------

[1. Leg Press (Narrow / Mid-Low Platform)]
  • **Order Rationale**: **#1 Primary Quad Mass Driver.** Heavy mechanical tension for the vastus lateralis quad sweep while nervous system is 100% fresh.
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 150 sec | Target RIR: **1–2**
  • **Target Weight**: **54.0 kg – 60.0 kg**
  • *Floor Target*: 54.0 kg × 12, 12, 12 reps (re-confirming Oct 21 ceiling).
  • *Main Target*: Set 1 @ 60.0 kg (8–10 reps); Sets 2–3 @ 54.0 kg (11–12 reps).
  • *Stretch Target*: Set 1 @ 60.0 kg (10–12 reps); Set 2 @ 60.0 kg (8–10 reps); Set 3 @ 54.0 kg (12 reps).
  • **Warm-Up Ramp**: 30.0 kg × 10 reps, 42.0 kg × 6 reps before working sets.
  • **Form Cue**: Feet placed hip-width apart, bottom-to-middle third of the platform. Grip handles firmly to pull your hips down tight into the seat. Smooth descent until knees reach 90°. Stop before lower back lifts off pad.
  • **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Drive through the mid-foot and outer ball of foot. Think about pushing the world away exclusively with the fronts of your thighs. Do NOT lock out knees violently at top.

[2. Leg Extension Machine]
  • **Order Rationale**: **#2 Quad Hypertrophy Finisher.** High metabolic stress and peak shortened-position contraction on the rectus femoris.
  • **Working Sets**: **3 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
  • **Target Weight**: **10.0 kg – 12.0 kg pin**
  • *Floor Target*: 10.0 kg × 12, 12, 12 reps.
  • *Main Target*: Set 1 @ 12.0 kg (8–10 reps); Sets 2–3 @ 10.0 kg (12–15 reps).
  • *Stretch Target*: 12.0 kg × 10, 10, 8 reps.
  • **Form Cue**: Align machine pivot point directly with the side of your knee joint. Back pad adjusted so knees sit flush against the seat edge without pinching.
  • **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Kick up smoothly, lock out fully, and violently flex your quads for 1 full second at the apex. Lower under strict 3-second negative control.

[3. Lying Leg Curl Machine]
  • **Order Rationale**: **#3 Hamstring Knee Flexion & Pelvic Restoration.** Directly counteracts shortened hip flexors and anterior pelvic tilt by strengthening the posterior chain knee flexors.
  • **Working Sets**: **4 sets x 10–12 reps** | Rest: 90 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
  • **Target Weight**: **5.0 kg – 7.5 kg**
  • *Floor Target*: 5.0 kg × 10, 10, 10, 10 reps.
  • *Main Target*: 5.0 kg × 12, 12, 12, 10 reps (conquering rep ceiling).
  • *Stretch Target*: Set 1 @ 7.5 kg (8–10 reps); Sets 2–4 @ 5.0 kg (12, 12, 12 reps).
  • **Form Cue**: Lie face down, knee caps just off the edge of the pad. Pad placed on lower calf/Achilles. **Dorsiflex ankles hard (pull toes upward toward your shins)** and keep hips pressed flat into the bench.
  • **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Pull heels toward glutes using only hamstring tension. Never lift your hips off the pad to cheat momentum. Pause 1s at top contraction, control the 3s lowering phase.

[4. Standing Calf Raise]
  • **Order Rationale**: **#4 Gastrocnemius Mass & Diamond Calf Development.** Full-range ankle plantarflexion under heavy load with zero Achilles elastic cheat.
  • **Working Sets**: **4 sets x 12–15 reps** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **0**
  • **Target Weight**: **80.0 kg – 85.0 kg pin (or machine/bodyweight deficit)**
  • *Floor Target*: 80.0 kg × 12, 12, 12, 12 reps.
  • *Main Target*: 85.0 kg × 12, 12, 12, 12 reps.
  • *Stretch Target*: 85.0 kg × 15, 15, 14, 12 reps.
  • **Form Cue**: Balls of feet on the edge of the block. Knees soft-locked with a tiny micro-bend.
  • **Mind-Muscle Cue**: Drop into the deepest possible heel stretch and **HOLD STILL FOR 2 FULL SECONDS** to eliminate elastic tendon bounce. Drive hard up onto your big toes, squeeze calves at the peak for 1 second.

[5. Bodyweight Floor Crunch & Deadbugs (Anti-APT Superset)]
  • **Order Rationale**: **#5 Transverse Abdominis & Rectus Abdominis Pelvic Tilt Reversal.** Stabilizes the lumbar spine, strengthens deep core wall, and pulls the anteriorly tilted pelvis back into alignment.
  • **Working Sets**: **3 supersets (12–15 crunches immediately into 6–8 deadbugs per side)** | Rest: 60 sec | Target RIR: **0–1**
  • **Target Weight**: **Bodyweight (0.0 kg)**
  • **Floor Crunch Execution**:
  • Lie on back, knees bent at 90°, feet flat on floor.
  • **Press lower back 100% flat into the floor (zero gap under lumbar spine)**.
  • Hands lightly touching temples. Curl your ribcage toward your pelvis; exhale every drop of air from your lungs at peak crunch. Hold peak contraction for 1 full second.
  • **Deadbug Execution**:
  • Immediately raise knees to 90° tabletop position, arms pointing straight up.
  • Maintain absolute lumbar contact with the ground.
  • Slowly extend right arm overhead while extending left leg straight out hovering 2 inches off floor. Return and alternate.
  • *CUE*: If lower back arches off floor, stop extension immediately. Quality over reach.

[6. Post-Workout Posture Reset: Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch]
  • **Execution**: 2 sets × 30 seconds per side.
  • **Form Cue**: Drop into half-kneeling lunge on mat. Squeeze rear glute hard to force posterior pelvic tilt. Keep torso tall and tuck pelvis under. Do NOT lean forward excessively at the lower back—the stretch must be felt deeply in the front of the hip (psoas/rectus femoris).
