### 3. Primary Limitation or Technical Flaw: Single-Arm Cable Row Biomechanical Breakdown
- **The Problem Identified**: Trainee reported: *"Felt or not felt MMC idk .. just felt kinda weird idk which muscle from I was pulling kinda all went tired and I was going and going in small can't pull in hard so took some time to find the weight suited for rep also I hard swing while seating make i shoulder a bit ouch"*.
- **The Anatomical Breakdown**:
  1. **Seated Instability & Torso Twisting**: When seated without chest support or bench bracing, pulling heavy unilateral load causes the torso to rotate and swing backward.
  2. **Shoulder Impingement / Anterior Capsule Strain**: Pulling with torso swing violently yanks the humerus into excessive extension and internal rotation at the turnaround, causing anterior shoulder pain ("ouch").
  3. **MMC Disconnection**: Without a stable anchor, the lat cannot pull with maximum force; instead, smaller stabilizing muscles (teres, forearms, rear delt) burn out prematurely.
  4. **Session Duration Inflation (101 mins)**: Spending 4 sets "hunting" for the right pin (12kg $\rightarrow$ 15kg $\rightarrow$ 20kg $\rightarrow$ 15kg) and resting between multiple experimental sets blew out session duration from 60 mins to 101 mins.

### 4. What Must Change: The Unilateral Lat Decision
- **Option A (Master the Movement with Strict Stability)**:
  - If retaining Single-Arm Cable Row:
    1. **Zero Torso Rotation**: Drop the load to a strict 12.5–15.0 kg pin.
    2. **Anchor Opposite Arm**: Brace the non-working arm firmly on a bench or knee. Lock ribs down.
    3. **Stop at the Waistband**: Drive elbow straight back toward the hip crease and **STOP when the elbow aligns with the torso**. Never pull the elbow behind the ribcage.
- **Option B (Recommended Biomechanical Upgrade - One-Arm DB Row on Bench)**:
  - Swap to **One-Arm Dumbbell Row with Knee & Hand Braced on Bench (15.0–17.5 kg DB)**. The three-point bench contact provides 100% rock-solid spinal stability, eliminates torso swinging, protects the shoulder completely, and gives an unmistakable, visceral lower lat stretch and contraction.
