### 3. Trainee Inquiry Analysis & Coaching Directives
- **Inquiry 1: Cable Crunch vs. Bodyweight Floor Crunch**:
  - *Coach Verdict*: You made the 100% correct autoregulation call. Cable crunches feel "cheat-y" for beginners because the hip flexors take over or you sit back onto your heels. Floor crunches with lower back pinned into the mat and a 2-second crunch at the peak contract the rectus abdominis with 100% pure core tension. Keep floor crunches as your primary anchor until core bracing strength is elevated.
- **Inquiry 2: Lying Leg Raises vs. Deadbugs for Lower Abs & APT**:
  - *Coach Verdict*: Lower abs are the lower fibers of the rectus abdominis and deep transverse abdominis. Lying leg raises can easily recruit the psoas (hip flexor), which pulls your pelvis into *more* anterior tilt if your lower back arches!
  - *Rule*: On Lying Leg Raises, you **must keep your lower back pressed flat into the floor at all times** (posterior pelvic tilt tuck). If your back arches even 1mm, stop the rep. **Deadbugs are superior** for your specific physique goal because they force transverse abdominis anti-extension bracing without hip flexor strain.
